What to check before you buy used

Grades give you a starting point. Before you commit, run through this checklist so the furniture fits your space and your team.

  • Measure the room, not just the footprint. Account for aisle widths, door swings and columns. Free space planning can catch conflicts before delivery.
  • Match quantities. Panels, worksurfaces and storage from the same product line fit together more cleanly. Ask whether enough matching components are available for your full count.
  • Confirm panel heights. Lower panels support collaboration and daylight. Taller panels give privacy and acoustic separation for phone-heavy teams.
  • Check chair adjustments. Seat height, tilt, arms and lumbar should all work. For seating, browse pre-owned office chairs by brand and grade.
  • Plan power and data. Know whether your stations need powered panels and where the building feeds come in.
  • Book the loading details. Ask your property manager about dock access, elevator reservations and any certificate of insurance requirements.
