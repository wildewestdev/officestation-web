Used Haworth Furniture

Pre-owned Haworth workstations and seating, graded A, B or C and sold by an independent reseller. Here is what to know about compatibility, condition and cost before you buy.

6 minute read

Haworth furniture is built for commercial offices that reconfigure often. Its panel systems are designed to be taken down, moved and put back up, and its task chairs are made for full workdays. Those same qualities make used Haworth furniture a practical buy. A system that has already survived one office move is likely to handle another.

Office Station sells pre-owned Haworth as an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer. Every piece is graded A, B or C for condition, so you can decide where to spend and where to save.

Haworth furniture you may find pre-owned

Haworth has produced several workstation systems and seating lines over the years. The pre-owned market reflects whatever offices are replacing, so availability shifts constantly. Names you may come across include:

  • Panel-based workstation systems such as Compose, UniGroup and Premise, used for cubicles with fabric or tackable panels, work surfaces, overhead storage and powered bases.
  • Task chairs such as Zody, Fern, Very and Soji, used at workstations and in private offices.
  • Supporting pieces like pedestals, lateral files, tables and freestanding desks that come out of the same offices.

Ask about current inventory rather than assuming a specific model is on hand. If you need an exact family to match what you already own, say so up front.

Compatibility: the most important question

With panel systems, compatibility matters more than brand. Two Haworth workstations can look similar and still use different connectors, panel heights, frame profiles and electrical harnesses.

  • Adding to an existing floor. Match the product family, not just the manufacturer. Photograph panel labels, connectors and the power base from your current workstations.
  • Furnishing a new floor. Compatibility with old furniture does not matter. Choose the layout, panel height and finish you want and buy it as a complete set.
  • Electrical. Powered panels need the right in-feeds, harnesses and receptacles, and a licensed electrician makes the connection to building power. Electrical work is not included in workstation pricing.
Do not mix families blindly. Haworth parts from one system generally will not connect to another, and components from other manufacturers will not connect either. Buying a complete, matched set of workstations avoids the most expensive surprise in used cubicles: a floor of panels that will not go together.

Which tier makes sense for you

The right tier depends less on budget alone and more on your timeline and how the space will be seen.

  • Choose liquidation-grade when you need seats fast, the floor is mostly internal, and grade B or C wear is acceptable in exchange for the lowest price.
  • Choose remanufactured when you want a more uniform, refreshed look across the floor and can wait a week or two.
  • Choose new when you need a specific finish or configuration that pre-owned inventory cannot match and a 4 to 6 week lead time fits your move date.

Many offices mix tiers, putting refreshed or new workstations in client-facing areas and liquidation-grade furniture in back-office rows. Free space planning can help you decide where each tier belongs.

Buying checklist for used Haworth

  1. Confirm the product family and whether you need it to match existing furniture.
  2. Count every component: panels, connectors, work surfaces, supports, pedestals and power parts.
  3. Ask for photos of panel fabric, surface edges and chair arms, where wear shows first.
  4. Test every chair adjustment: height, tilt, tilt lock, arms and lumbar where equipped.
  5. Confirm panel height works for your privacy needs and sightlines.
  6. Plan electrical early so an electrician is booked when installation finishes.
  7. Remember that manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture.

Frequently asked questions

Is Office Station an authorized Haworth dealer?

No. Office Station is an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture and is not an authorized dealer for Haworth or any other manufacturer. Haworth is named here only to describe the furniture being resold.

Does a Haworth warranty come with used furniture?

Generally no. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, so you should not count on manufacturer coverage. Review the condition grade and ask questions about any piece before you buy.

Can I add used Haworth panels to my existing Haworth cubicles?

Sometimes, but only within a compatible product family. Different Haworth panel systems use different connectors, heights and electrical components, so parts from one family usually do not work with another. Share photos and panel labels from your current setup so compatibility can be checked first.

How fast can I get used Haworth workstations?
