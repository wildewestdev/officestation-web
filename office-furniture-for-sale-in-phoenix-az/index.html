Three price tiers for Phoenix buyers

Every tier below is a 6'x6' workstation, furniture only. Electrical, delivery and professional installation are priced separately.

Liquidation-grade: $615 per station

Pre-owned stations recovered from corporate offices. This is the fastest option, available in as little as 72 hours when in stock. It suits startups, call centers, overflow seating and any office that needs to be working next week.

Remanufactured: $1,245 per station

Pre-owned stations refurbished before delivery, usually ready in 7 to 14 days. A good middle ground when you want a consistent look without the wait or cost of new.

New: $1,920 per station

Built to your specification, with 4 to 6 weeks of lead time. The right call when you have brand standards to meet and a longer runway before move-in.

72 hours is a stock question. Liquidation-grade availability depends on what is in stock in the configuration and quantity you need. If your date is firm, share your station count early so you can lock in stock before someone else does, or plan a fallback on remanufactured.

Choosing a tier by your Phoenix timeline

Most Phoenix buyers can work backward from a single date. Here is how that plays out:

  • Move-in in under two weeks: start with liquidation-grade stock. Remanufactured may still work at the short end of its 7 to 14 day window, but do not count on it.
  • Move-in in two to four weeks: remanufactured is realistic, and you can still fill gaps with liquidation-grade if a configuration is short.
  • Move-in in six weeks or more: new is on the table, along with either pre-owned tier if you would rather keep the savings.

Remember to leave time after installation for the electrician. A station that is installed but not powered is not ready for a Monday morning.

Condition grades in practice

The grade matters most where people look closely. Reception desks, conference rooms and executive offices are where scuffs and mismatched finishes stand out. Open workstation areas, especially in large rows, are more forgiving. If a pre-owned group mixes grades, ask how they will be placed so the best pieces land where they are seen.

Phoenix buying checklist

  • Headcount now and in 12 months, so volume pricing and layout reflect real plans
  • Move-in date, which decides your realistic tier
  • Free space plan for station count and aisle clearance
  • Building rules: dock times, freight elevator bookings and after-hours requirements, which are common in multi-tenant towers
  • Certificate of insurance naming whatever holder your property manager requires
  • Electrician scheduled after install for powered stations
  • Condition grade confirmed for each pre-owned item
