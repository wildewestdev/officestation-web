The honest warranty conversation

This is the part many buyers skip, and it is the part that matters most with premium brands.

Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer for any of the brands above. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. In practical terms, if you buy a pre-owned chair from a well-known brand, you should not plan on filing a claim with that manufacturer if a mechanism fails later.

That does not make pre-owned a bad deal. It changes how you should buy:

  • Put more weight on the condition grade than on the brand name.
  • Inspect the pieces that move: chair tilt and height mechanisms, arm adjustments, casters, drawer slides and height-adjustable table motors.
  • Price the savings against the warranty you are giving up. If a specific item's value to you depends on long-term manufacturer coverage, buying new may be the better choice for that item.
Ask the direct question. Before you order, ask what applies to the specific pieces on your quote. Get the condition grade in writing for each line. A reseller who is clear about what is not covered is easier to plan around than one who is vague about what is.

Matching condition grade to the room

Every pre-owned piece carries a grade. Here is a practical way to use those grades across a typical Scottsdale office.

GradeWhat it meansWhere it usually fitsWhat to check
ALike newReception, conference rooms, executive officesFinish consistency across matching pieces
BVery goodOpen workstations, private offices clients may seeVisible wear on work surfaces and arm pads
CGoodBack office, training rooms, storage areasFunction of every moving part

Mixing grades by room is one of the easiest ways to save. Your conference room can look flawless while the file room uses perfectly functional grade C storage.

Frequently asked questions

Is Office Station an authorized Herman Miller or Steelcase dealer?

No. Office Station sells pre-owned Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth, Humanscale, Teknion, Allsteel, Kimball, AIS and Friant furniture as an independent reseller. It is not an authorized dealer for any of those manufacturers.

Does the manufacturer warranty come with used designer furniture?

Generally, no. Manufacturer warranties usually do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, so you should not count on the original warranty. That is why condition grades and your own inspection matter more when buying used.

What do the A, B and C condition grades mean?

Grade A means like new, grade B means very good and grade C means good. For client-facing rooms in a Scottsdale office, most buyers look at A or B, while C can be a smart value for back-office or training areas.
