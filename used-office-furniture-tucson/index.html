Used Office Furniture in Tucson

Tucson businesses can both sell and buy used office furniture through Office Station. Liquidation walkthroughs typically happen within 2 to 3 business days, and pre-owned furniture ships with freight quoted for your project.

6 minute read

Tucson offices tend to face two furniture questions at different moments. When a lease ends, a team consolidates or a location closes, the question is what to do with everything left behind. When a team grows or opens a new suite, the question is how to furnish it without paying new prices. Office Station handles both for Tucson.

Selling or clearing furniture from a Tucson office

Tucson is one of Office Station's liquidation service markets. The process is built around a written number and a clean handback, so you can plan your exit with fewer surprises.

How a Tucson liquidation works

  1. Request a walkthrough. In Tucson, walkthroughs typically happen within 2 to 3 business days.
  2. Receive a written net offer. Within 5 business days, you get an offer equal to the resale value of the furniture minus removal cost. Positive means you are paid.
  3. Schedule removal. Crews can work nights or weekends so the office keeps running until the last day. Certificates of insurance, freight elevator reservations and dock time are coordinated with your building.
  4. Document everything. You receive an itemized inventory, certificates of data destruction for e-waste, and a diversion summary showing where material went.
  5. Hand back the space. The suite is left broom-clean with photo documentation you can share with your landlord.

Start before the lease clock gets tight. With a 2 to 3 business day walkthrough and up to 5 business days for the offer, allow at least two weeks before your required move-out date, and more if your building limits after-hours freight elevator access.

Selling old furniture and buying replacements

Some Tucson projects involve both sides at once. A team moves to a smaller or newer suite, the old furniture no longer fits, and the new space needs workstations in a different footprint. Handling both through one conversation keeps the timeline aligned.

A practical sequence looks like this:

  1. Book the liquidation walkthrough of the current office and get the written net offer.
  2. Share the new floor plan for free space planning and a workstation quote with Tucson freight.
  3. Choose options that fit the move date. If the new suite must be ready in days, favor in-stock liquidation-grade pieces; if you have a few weeks, remanufactured may fit.
  4. Schedule removal from the old space after the team has moved, so nobody works out of boxes.

A positive net offer on the outgoing furniture can offset part of the cost of furnishing the new space.

Tucson project checklist

  • Note your lease end date and any landlord restoration requirements.
  • Ask building management about dock hours, elevator reservations and COI requirements.
  • Have IT tag any equipment to keep before e-waste is scheduled.
  • Take a quick photo set of the furniture to speed up the first conversation.
  • If buying, share a floor plan so free space planning can confirm station counts.
  • Decide who handles electrical for new stations, since it is not included.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you do a liquidation walkthrough in Tucson?

Walkthroughs outside Greater Phoenix, including Tucson, typically happen within 2 to 3 business days of your request. A written net offer usually follows within 5 business days of the walkthrough. Share your lease deadline up front so the schedule works backward from it.

What is a net offer?

A net offer is the resale value of your furniture minus the cost to remove it. If resale value is higher than removal cost, the number is positive and Office Station pays you. If it is negative, the offer shows what the removal would cost you, in writing, before you commit.

Do you deliver used cubicles to Tucson?

Yes, as a quoted freight project. Zone delivery pricing of $195 or $295 applies only in Greater Phoenix. For Tucson, freight is quoted per project based on quantity and destination.

What happens to computers and electronics left in the office?

E-waste is handled as part of the project, with certificates of data destruction provided for devices that store data. Make sure IT has identified any equipment that must be retained before removal day.

Is pre-owned furniture covered by the manufacturer warranty?

Generally no. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer, and manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Condition grades of A, B or C tell you what to expect.
