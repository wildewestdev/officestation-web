4 Moving Tips for Coordinating Commercial Furniture in Your West Valley Office

Moving an office in Glendale, Peoria or elsewhere in the West Valley goes smoother when the furniture plan is settled early. These four tips cover what to move, what to sell, and how to keep building managers and your team on your side.

6 minute read

An office move in the West Valley has a lot of moving parts, and furniture is usually the biggest. Whether you are relocating from a Glendale suite to a larger space in Peoria, consolidating two locations or downsizing into a smaller footprint, the same problems come up: furniture that does not fit the new floor plan, buildings with strict access rules and a team that still needs to work during the transition. These four office furniture moving tips will help you plan it in the right order.

Tip 1: Decide what moves before you book anything

The most expensive mistake in a commercial move is paying to haul furniture that does not fit the new space. Before you schedule movers, get the new floor plan and compare it to what you own.

  • Measure workstations, not just rooms. Cubicles that worked in an old layout may not fit around new columns, windows or electrical locations.
  • Check finish and condition. Moving worn furniture into a fresh space often means replacing it six months later anyway.
  • Look at headcount plans. If the team is growing, you may need more seats than you have. If you are going hybrid, you may need fewer.
  • Tag everything. Label each item as move, sell or dispose so crews are not guessing on move day.

Tip 3: Get building logistics in writing early

Commercial buildings have rules, and both your old and new property managers will have their own. Ignoring them can stall a move at the loading dock. Call both buildings early and ask:

Ask the buildingWhy it matters
What are the COI requirements?Crews usually cannot enter without a certificate of insurance naming the right parties.
When can we reserve the freight elevator?Many buildings restrict freight use during business hours.
Is there a loading dock, and how is it scheduled?Shared docks get booked by other tenants and deliveries.
Are nights or weekends allowed?After-hours work often avoids tenant conflicts.
What floor and wall protection is required?Some buildings require protective coverings in common areas.
What condition must the old suite be left in?Your lease may require a broom-clean return to avoid charges.

Office Station coordinates COIs, freight elevator reservations and dock scheduling as part of the project, so you are not relaying messages between crews and property managers.

Plan around the heat. West Valley summers are hot, and loading docks and parking lots get uncomfortable for crews and hard on some materials during the afternoon. Early mornings, evenings and weekends are often the most practical windows for loading and unloading, which is one more reason to line up after-hours building access in advance.

Tip 4: Schedule so your team never stops working

Downtime is a hidden cost of every move. The goal is for people to leave the old office on a Friday and sit down at working desks in the new one on Monday.

  1. Install new furniture first. If you are adding workstations, have them delivered and installed in the new space before the move date.
  2. Move people and equipment on nights or weekends. Office Station crews can work after hours at both locations.
  3. Clear the old space last. Once everyone is settled, liquidation crews remove what stays behind and hand the suite back broom clean.
  4. Confirm power and data. Workstation furniture pricing excludes electrical, so have your electrician and IT team scheduled before the move date.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I plan a West Valley office move?

Start the furniture plan as soon as your new lease is signed. That leaves time to measure the new space, decide what to keep, get a liquidation offer on the rest and order anything new or pre-owned. In-stock pre-owned workstations can be ready in as little as 72 hours, but new furniture typically takes 4 to 6 weeks.

What is a COI and why does my building ask for one?

A certificate of insurance shows that the crew working in the building carries liability coverage. Most commercial property managers require one before movers or installers can use loading docks, elevators or common areas. Request your building's exact COI requirements early, because they often specify named insureds and coverage amounts.

Can the move happen after business hours?

Yes. Office Station crews can work nights and weekends, which helps keep your team productive and is often preferred by buildings that restrict freight elevator use during the workday. Coordinate the schedule with both your old and new property managers.
