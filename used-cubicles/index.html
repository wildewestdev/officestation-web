How this works

Ask for a style, not a SKU

You rarely need an exact match. You need stations that look right, fit the plan and arrive on time. Tell us the system you like and we'll work the liquidation pipeline for it:

  1. In stock now. If we have that system in the quantity you need, you get a number the same day and installation in as little as 72 hours.
  2. Sourced from an incoming liquidation. We know what is coming out of floors before it hits the market. Most matched runs come from here.
  3. Remanufactured to match. Same frames, new fabric, new surfaces and current finishes, usually 7 to 14 days.
  4. The closest equivalent. If your system is genuinely scarce, we'll tell you which one looks and performs closest instead of stringing you along.

Not sure what you already have? Send a photo of a panel connector and a worksurface edge and we will identify it.

    Questions

    Cubicle FAQ

    How much do used cubicles cost?

    Office Station's liquidation-grade 6x6 workstations start around $615 each, remanufactured around $1,245, and new commercial lines around $1,920, Furniture prices exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation, and space planning is free. The quote builder prices your exact layout.

    What's the difference between used and remanufactured cubicles?

    Liquidation-grade cubicles are cleaned, inspected and fully working, with normal wear. Remanufactured cubicles get new fabric, worksurfaces and edge banding, so they look close to new at about two-thirds the price of new.

    How fast can cubicles be installed?

    In-stock liquidation-grade workstations can be installed in as little as 72 hours in the Phoenix metro. Remanufactured workstations usually take 7 to 14 days, and new furniture typically takes 4 to 6 weeks.

    Do you offer volume pricing?

    Yes: 5% off furniture at 20 or more workstations, 8% at 50 or more and 12% at 100 or more.

    Can you match cubicles to what we already have?

    Often, yes. Liquidations produce large runs of popular systems, so adding matching stations to an existing layout is a common request. Send the manufacturer and model with your inquiry.
