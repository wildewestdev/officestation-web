Gilbert offices can price pre-owned workstations in three clear tiers, then layer on volume discounts and flat metro delivery. Here is how the numbers work before you ask for a quote.

Watch the thresholds

The break points create a few quirks worth knowing. At liquidation-grade pricing, 19 stations with no discount cost $11,685, exactly the same as 20 stations with the 5% discount. Likewise, 46 undiscounted stations cost the same as 50 discounted ones, and 88 cost the same as 100.

That does not mean you should buy furniture you will not use. It does mean that if your Gilbert team is at 18 or 19 seats and you have a hire or two planned this year, the extra station may cost you little or nothing.

Budget tip. Build your quote at your current headcount and at your 12-month headcount. If the second number crosses a volume threshold, the per-station difference can make a spare station or two very cheap insurance against a second delivery later.

Delivery and installation in Gilbert

Gilbert sits inside the Greater Phoenix delivery area, so you get flat zone pricing instead of a freight quote. Delivery is $195 in the central zone or $295 in the metro zone. Your street address sets the zone, and it is confirmed on your written quote.

Professional installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum. On a 20-station order that is $1,900. On a four-station order, the minimum applies and installation is $495.

A full example for a 20-station liquidation-grade order with installation:

  • Furniture after 5% volume pricing: $11,685
  • Installation, 20 stations at $95: $1,900
  • Delivery: $195 or $295 depending on zone
  • Estimated total before electrical: $13,780 to $13,880

Electrical is not included in any tier. If your stations need power feeds or base-in connections, plan to have a licensed electrician involved, and ask your landlord early whether the building has requirements for who performs that work.

Brands, grades and warranty expectations

Pre-owned inventory comes from brands such as Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth, Humanscale, Teknion, Allsteel, Kimball, AIS and Friant. Office Station sells these as an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, so the condition grade is the thing to pay attention to.

Before you order, check these points:

  1. Grade per line item. Ask for the A, B or C grade on each component, not just a general description.
  2. Panel heights. Taller panels add privacy but block light and sightlines. Mixing heights within one run can look uneven.
  3. Worksurface depth and shape. Corner and straight surfaces fit different work styles and monitor setups.
  4. Storage. Confirm whether pedestals or overheads are included in the station you are pricing.
  5. Matching future orders. If you expect to add stations later, ask how easy it will be to source more of the same product line.
