New or pre-owned?

New storage makes sense when you need a current finish, a specific model, a full manufacturer warranty, or matching product you cannot source used. Budget-wise, new typically runs about 4 to 6 weeks from an approved order.

Where the same job can be done with inventory already built, we will say so. Liquidation-grade workstations install in as little as 72 hours and remanufactured usually in 7 to 14 days, at a fraction of new. A lot of good projects put new where it shows and pre-owned everywhere else. See cubicle configurations for layouts and the quote builder for pre-owned pricing.
