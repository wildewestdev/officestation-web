Categories Trinity covers

Within our 16-category range, Trinity applies to the following. Each links to what to include in an RFQ for that category.

  • Seating — task, executive, conference, guest and lounge
  • Tables — conference, training, café and occasional

Getting a number back

Send quantities, your delivery city and the date the space has to work. A floor plan helps but a sketch with dimensions is fine. Space planning is free, and the quote comes back with freight, delivery and professional installation included rather than bolted on later.

If you are replacing existing furniture, tell us. The outgoing furniture usually has resale value and we credit it against the order instead of charging you to haul it away. That is the liquidation side of the business, and it is why our net number often beats a furniture-only quote from a dealer.

New or pre-owned

New Trinity product is the right call when you need a current finish, a full manufacturer warranty or a specific model. Where the same job can be done with inventory already built, we will say so: see configurations and pre-owned pricing. Most good projects use both.

Trinity FAQ

Can Office Station quote Trinity?

Yes. Trinity is on the line card we quote through our manufacturer-rep partner network, covering Arizona, California, Nevada, Colorado, New Mexico, Utah and the wider West. Send quantities, your delivery city and the date the space has to be usable, and we return a written quote including freight, delivery and installation.

What does Trinity make?

Seating and tables for corporate, hospitality and common-area applications. On our side that maps to seating, tables.

How long does a Trinity order take?

New contract furniture typically runs 4 to 6 weeks from an approved order, longer for custom finishes or COM upholstery. If your date will not survive that, we will price a mix: in-stock liquidation-grade installs in as little as 72 hours and remanufactured usually in 7 to 14 days.

Is Office Station an authorized Trinity dealer?

Office Station is an independent reseller. Brand names identify the manufacturer of the product being quoted. Any dealer authorization, warranty or program terms that apply to your order are stated in writing on the quote.
