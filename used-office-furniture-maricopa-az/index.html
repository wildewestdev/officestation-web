Used Office Furniture in Maricopa, AZ

Maricopa offices are often small, growing and on a deadline. Here is how to choose pre-owned furniture around your opening date and how delivery pricing works for a Maricopa address.

6 minute read

Maricopa, AZ has its own business community south of the Phoenix metro, and plenty of the offices you see there are small: a clinic front desk, an insurance or real estate office, a property management team, a startup that just outgrew a spare bedroom. If you are shopping for used office furniture in Maricopa, the questions tend to be practical. What can I get before we open? What will delivery cost to my address? Where should a limited budget go first?

This page answers those questions honestly, including how delivery pricing is determined for a Maricopa location.

How delivery pricing works for a Maricopa address

Office Station publishes two flat delivery rates for Greater Phoenix: $195 for the central zone and $295 for the metro zone. Because Maricopa sits farther from the center of the Valley than many metro cities, the zone for your specific address is checked when you request a quote. If your address falls outside the Greater Phoenix zones, freight is quoted per project instead.

Either way, you see the delivery cost on a written quote before you commit, so you can budget with the full number in hand.

Ask for the zone first. When you reach out, include your full street address along with your item list. Knowing whether you are paying $195, $295 or a project freight quote lets you finish your budget in one pass instead of two.

Where a small office budget should go first

A five-person office does not need to furnish every room on day one. Spend first on what people touch all day, then fill in the rest.

  1. Chairs. A supportive task chair affects comfort more than any other piece. Browse pre-owned office chairs from brands like Herman Miller, Steelcase and Humanscale.
  2. Worksurfaces. Workstations or desks sized for how each person actually works, with room for monitors.
  3. Storage. Pedestals or lateral files so paperwork does not live on desks.
  4. Front desk or reception. Important if clients or patients visit, less so for back-office teams.
  5. Meeting and break areas. Often the easiest to add later.

Pre-owned pieces are graded A like new, B very good or C good. For a client-facing front office, A-grade may be worth it. For private work areas, B or C grade can stretch the budget without affecting function.

A sample Maricopa small-office budget

Here is how a six-station office could price out using remanufactured workstations with professional installation:

  • Furniture: 6 x $1,245 = $7,470
  • Installation: 6 x $95 = $570, which is above the $495 minimum
  • Delivery: $195 or $295 if your address is in a Greater Phoenix zone, or a per-project freight quote if it is not
  • Electrical: not included

The same six stations in liquidation-grade would be $3,690 in furniture, a savings of $3,780 if you can work with in-stock pieces and their grades. Chairs, files and reception pieces would be added to the same quote. Free space planning helps confirm that six stations is the right number for your floor plan before you order.

What to know about brands and warranty

Office Station sells pre-owned furniture from Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth, Humanscale, Teknion, Allsteel, Kimball, AIS and Friant as an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Before buying, check:

  • The condition grade listed for each piece
  • Chair mechanisms, casters and armrest adjustments
  • Drawer slides and locks on storage, and whether keys are included
  • Worksurface edges and panel fabric in high-wear spots

Planning for growth

A growing team can outpace its first layout. If you expect to grow, ask about how easy it will be to add matching stations later, and consider whether a slightly larger initial order is sensible. Volume pricing starts at 20 stations with 5% off, then 8% at 50 and 12% at 100, which matters more for larger teams than for a first small office.
