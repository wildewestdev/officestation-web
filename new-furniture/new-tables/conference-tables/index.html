Conference Tables Sized for Your Room

The right conference table fits the room, the people and the technology. We source conference tables in new and pre-owned designer lines and plan the room with you for free.

7 minute read

Size the table to the room, not the other way around

Conference tables are the piece most often bought too big. A table that looks right in a catalog can leave no room to push back a chair once the meeting is full. Start with the room.

  1. Measure the room. Record length and width, plus doors, windows, columns and any built-in credenza.
  2. Subtract clearance. Leave enough space on every side for a person to sit with the chair pushed back and for someone else to walk behind. Plan more clearance on the side with the door.
  3. Set the seat count. Allow roughly 30 inches of table edge per person for comfortable seating.
  4. Place the screen. If one end holds a display, keep that end clear of chairs and check that everyone can see it without twisting.
  5. Find the power. Note where floor boxes or outlets are. The table's power module should line up with them.

Plan for the technology

Most meetings now include a laptop, a display or a video call. The table has to support that without cables sprawled across the top.

  • Power and data modules. Flip-up or pop-up modules keep outlets at the table. Confirm the module type and that it works before buying a pre-owned table.
  • Cable management. Look for cable troughs under the top and a path down through the base to the floor.
  • Camera sightlines. For video calls, boat and tapered tables help the camera see every seat.
  • Electrical. Our prices exclude electrical work. If the table needs a new floor box, plan it before installation day.

What to inspect on a pre-owned conference table

  • Top surface free of deep scratches, water rings, burns or veneer lifting
  • Edges intact, with no chipped banding at the corners
  • All sections included for multi-piece tables, with matching finish
  • Connecting hardware and brackets present
  • Bases stable, with leveling glides that adjust
  • Power modules open, close and deliver power
  • Overall dimensions measured and checked against your plan
  • A path into the room: elevator, hallway and door sizes that the table or its sections can clear

Frequently asked questions

What size conference table fits my room?

Start with the room dimensions and leave generous clearance on every side for chairs to pull out and people to walk behind them. A table that fills the room edge to edge will feel cramped the first time the meeting is full. Send us your dimensions and we will size it for free.

How many people fit at a conference table?

A common planning figure is about 30 inches of table edge per person for comfortable seating. Tighter spacing works for short meetings but gets uncomfortable with laptops and notebooks. Count the people you need to seat regularly, not the rare all-hands.

Can I get a pre-owned designer conference table?

Yes. We sell pre-owned furniture from lines including Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth and Teknion as an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned pieces, so each one carries a condition grade.

How long does a conference table take?

Liquidation-grade tables can be ready in as little as 72 hours when in stock. Remanufactured tables usually take 7-14 days, and new tables typically take 4-6 weeks.

Do you install conference tables and handle the power?

We can quote installation with your table. Large tables often arrive in sections and need careful joining and leveling. Prices exclude electrical work, so coordinate floor boxes or new circuits with your electrician.
