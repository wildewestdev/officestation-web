New Office Cubicles, Priced Next to the Alternatives

New cubicles are the right call for some offices and an expensive habit for others. See new, remanufactured and liquidation-grade 6'x6' stations side by side, with real prices, lead times and project math.

If you are pricing new office cubicles, you are probably comparing quotes that bundle furniture, delivery, installation and sometimes electrical into one number. That makes it hard to see what you are actually paying for. When new cubicles are worth the premium

  • You need specific finishes. Brand colors, a particular fabric or a matching laminate across several floors are easiest with new.
  • You are filling a very large, uniform floor. New components make it easiest for every station to match.
  • Your timeline has room. If move-in is two months out, a 4 to 6 week lead time works.
  • You want warranty coverage. Ask about coverage for the specific new product. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture.

When remanufactured or liquidation-grade is the smarter buy

  • Your lease starts soon. In-stock liquidation-grade stations can be ready in as little as 72 hours, and remanufactured usually takes 7 to 14 days.
  • Budget drives the decision. The difference between new and liquidation-grade on 50 stations is about $60,000 in furniture alone.
  • You are growing in stages. Lower-cost stations make it easier to add seats as you hire.
  • You care about reuse. Pre-owned systems keep usable panels and worksurfaces in service.

Pre-owned stations come from brands such as Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth, Teknion, Allsteel, Kimball, AIS and Friant. Office Station sells them as an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer.

Mixing tiers on one floor

You do not have to pick one tier for the whole office. A common approach is to put new or remanufactured stations in the most visible areas and liquidation-grade stations in back-office zones. Free space planning helps you lay this out so panel heights and finishes still read as one design.

Costs that do not show up on a furniture quote

The per-station price is only one line of the budget. When you compare quotes for new office cubicles, make sure each one accounts for the same items, or ask the vendor to separate them.

  • Electrical and data. Powered panels still need a licensed electrician to connect them to the building. This is priced separately from furniture on every tier.
  • Delivery and freight. In Greater Phoenix, delivery is a flat $195 central or $295 metro. Outside the Valley, freight is quoted per project and depends on distance and volume.
  • Building requirements. Many landlords require certificates of insurance, freight elevator reservations and after-hours work. Office Station coordinates COIs, docks and elevators and can schedule night or weekend crews.
  • Removal of old furniture. If existing stations must come out first, that work belongs in the plan too.
  • Accessories. Monitor arms, keyboard trays, task lights and pedestals are often quoted as extras. Confirm what each station includes.

Lining these up side by side is often what reveals the true gap between a new cubicle quote and a remanufactured one.

Before you order cubicles, check these

  1. Measure the space and note columns, windows and exits.
  2. Confirm station size. This page uses 6'x6' pricing.
  3. Decide panel heights for privacy and light.
  4. Plan power and data early, since electrical is a separate trade.
  5. Confirm building rules for deliveries, docks, elevators and after-hours work.
  6. Match lead time to your move-in date.
  7. Count stations to see which volume tier you reach.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a new office cubicle cost?

A new 6'x6' workstation from Office Station is $1,920 for the furniture only. That price excludes electrical, delivery and professional installation. Remanufactured stations of the same size are $1,245 and liquidation-grade stations are $615.

How long do new cubicles take?

New workstations typically take 4 to 6 weeks. Remanufactured stations usually take 7 to 14 days, and liquidation-grade stations can be ready in as little as 72 hours when in stock.

Is installation included in the cubicle price?

No. Professional installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum. Greater Phoenix delivery is $195 for the central zone or $295 for the wider metro, and freight elsewhere in the country is quoted per project. Electrical work is separate.

Do you offer discounts on large cubicle orders?

Yes. Volume pricing is 5 percent off at 20 or more stations, 8 percent at 50 or more and 12 percent at 100 or more. Free space planning is included when you work with us on a layout.

When should I choose new cubicles over remanufactured?

Choose new when you need specific finishes or fabrics, a perfectly uniform large floor or a timeline that allows 4 to 6 weeks. If speed or budget drives the project, remanufactured and liquidation-grade stations deliver the same footprint for far less.
