Privacy policy

What we collect when you use this site, and what we do with it.

Information you give us

When you submit a form we collect what you enter, such as name, company, email, phone, project details and estimator or quote selections. We use it to respond, prepare offers and quotes, and follow up about your project.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider records standard server logs. If analytics are enabled, they record pages visited and general device information to help us improve the site.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate, such as hosting and email delivery, and with subcontractors performing your project, or when required by law.

Email

If you request inventory alerts, we email you about inventory. Every message includes a way to opt out.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by replying to any email from us or through the contact form.

Updates

We may update this policy and will post the revised version here.
