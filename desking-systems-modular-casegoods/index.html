Used Benching and Desking Systems

Pre-owned benching and modular desking lets you open up a floor plan for far less than new. Here is how grades, lead times and volume pricing work so you can plan with real numbers.

6 minute read

What benching and modular desking actually are

Benching is a shared-spine desking system. Instead of each person getting a freestanding desk or a paneled cubicle, several work surfaces attach to a common beam or leg structure. Power and data run down the middle, and a low divider or screen separates people who face each other.

Modular desking is the broader family. It includes benching, but also height-adjustable desk runs, L-shaped returns, team tables and the casegoods that go with them: pedestals, lateral files, storage towers, credenzas and lockers. The point of a modular system is that you can add a seat, remove a seat or reconfigure a run without buying a whole new setup.

If you are shopping for used benching desking systems, you are usually trying to do one of three things: fit more people into the same square footage, open up a floor that feels closed in by tall panels, or furnish a new space quickly without paying new-furniture prices.

How condition grades work

Every piece we sell is graded so you can compare options on the same scale. Grades describe cosmetic condition and function, not brand or age.

  • Grade A, like new. Minimal to no visible wear. A good fit for client-facing areas and executive rows.
  • Grade B, very good. Light signs of use, such as small scuffs on legs or edges. Fully functional. This is where most teams find the best value.
  • Grade C, good. Noticeable wear, but structurally sound and working. Ideal for training rooms, back-office rows and budget-first projects.

Mixing grades is common and smart. Put Grade A near the entry and conference rooms, then use Grade B or C across the main floor where screens and monitors hide most of the surface anyway.

Brands we source

Benching and desking from established manufacturers holds up well on the secondary market because the frames, beams and hardware are built for years of reconfiguration. We regularly source desking and casegoods from Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth, Teknion, Allsteel, Kimball, AIS and Friant, along with sit-stand and accessory pieces from Humanscale.

One practical tip: try to keep a single run of benching within one brand and product line. Beams, end legs and screen clamps are rarely interchangeable across systems, so matching parts later is much easier if the whole run came from the same family.

What to check before you buy used benching

Run through this list before you commit to a system. It prevents the most common surprises on install day.

  1. Seat count per run. Confirm whether you need 4, 6, 8 or longer runs, and whether runs are single-sided against a wall or double-sided in the open.
  2. Work surface size. Common depths and widths vary by system. Make sure two monitors and a laptop fit comfortably.
  3. Screens and dividers. Check height, material and whether they are included with the run.
  4. Power path. Know where power enters each run and whether in-beam power modules are part of the package.
  5. Cable management. Trays, spines and grommets should be complete. Missing cable parts are the most common gap on secondary-market desking.
  6. Storage. Decide if pedestals sit under the surface, roll out, or are replaced by shared lockers or towers.
  7. Height adjustment. If the desks are sit-stand, test each motor and controller and confirm the height range.
  8. Future growth. Ask whether extra add-on segments are available in the same line so you can extend a run later.

Casegoods that pair with desking

Open desking works best when storage is planned at the same time. Lateral files and storage towers can act as soft walls between teams. Personal lockers free up the surface for hybrid seating. Yes. Our liquidation service can issue a written net offer on your existing furniture, which is resale value minus removal cost. If the result is positive, you get paid, and it can be coordinated with your install.
