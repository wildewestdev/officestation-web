Office Furniture in Phoenix, Priced in Plain Numbers

Compare liquidation-grade, remanufactured and new workstations side by side, with Valley delivery rates and lead times listed up front. No guessing what a Phoenix office will actually cost to furnish.

6 minute read

Shopping for office furniture in Phoenix usually means collecting three quotes that are impossible to compare. One includes install, one leaves out delivery, one prices a different panel height. This page puts the numbers that matter for a Valley office in one place so you can budget before you ever talk to anyone.

Why the 72-hour lead time matters in Phoenix

Phoenix leasing moves fast. Tenants sign on short notice, subleases open up mid-quarter and growing teams outrun their suites. When a lease starts in two weeks, a 4 to 6 week new-furniture lead time leaves staff working off folding tables.

In-stock liquidation-grade workstations can be ready in as little as 72 hours. That makes them the practical choice when your move-in date is fixed and close. If you have a month or more, remanufactured stations give you a more uniform finish without the new-furniture wait.

Check stock before you sign. The 72-hour window applies to workstations that are in stock for your quantity. Before you commit to a move-in date, confirm the count, configuration and panel height you need are actually available.

Delivery, installation and planning costs in the Valley

These are the line items that usually surprise buyers. Here is how they work for Greater Phoenix projects.

  • Delivery: $195 for the central zone, $295 for the wider metro zone. Outside Greater Phoenix, freight is quoted per project.
  • Installation: $95 per workstation, with a $495 minimum. A five-station install hits the minimum; a 30-station install is $2,850.
  • Space planning: free. Send a floor plan or rough dimensions and get a layout before you buy.
  • Electrical: not included. If your stations need powered panels hardwired to the building, plan for a licensed electrician through your landlord or contractor.

Volume pricing

Larger Phoenix build-outs qualify for volume pricing on workstation furniture: 5% off at 20 or more stations, 8% at 50 or more and 12% at 100 or more. At 100 remanufactured stations, that 12% takes $14,940 off the furniture total.

A sample budget for a 20-person Phoenix office

Here is how a straightforward order might look using liquidation-grade stations delivered to the central zone.

  1. 20 liquidation-grade 6'x6' workstations at $615: $12,300
  2. Volume pricing at 5%: minus $615, bringing furniture to $11,685
  3. Installation, 20 stations at $95: $1,900
  4. Central-zone delivery: $195
  5. Space planning: $0

That lands at $13,780 before electrical and tax. Run the same count in remanufactured and the furniture line becomes $23,655 after the 5% discount. Seeing both totals side by side makes the tier decision much easier.

Moving out of a Phoenix office instead?

Phoenix is also an Office Station liquidation market. If you are downsizing, consolidating or relocating, walkthroughs are typically scheduled within 48 hours in Greater Phoenix, and you get a written net offer within 5 business days. Frequently asked questions

How fast can I get office furniture in Phoenix?

Liquidation-grade workstations that are in stock can be ready in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured stations usually take 7 to 14 days, and new stations run 4 to 6 weeks. Confirm availability for your exact count before you set a move-in date.

What does delivery cost in Greater Phoenix?

Delivery is a flat $195 for the central zone and $295 for the wider metro zone. Your quote will show which zone your address falls in. Projects outside Greater Phoenix get freight quoted per project.

Do the workstation prices include installation?

No. The listed prices are for furniture only and exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation. Installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum, and space planning is free.

Is there a discount for furnishing a whole floor?

Yes. Volume pricing takes 5% off at 20 or more stations, 8% at 50 or more and 12% at 100 or more. The discount applies to the workstation furniture price.

Can Office Station also clear out my old Phoenix office?

Yes. Phoenix is one of the liquidation service markets, and walkthroughs are typically scheduled within 48 hours in Greater Phoenix. You receive a written net offer within 5 business days showing resale value minus removal cost.
