The 2026 Arizona Office Furniture Buying Guide

Liquidation-grade, remanufactured or new: each tier trades price against lead time and finish. This guide puts real 2026 numbers on all three so you can budget an Arizona office with confidence.

7 minute read

Buying office furniture in Arizona in 2026 comes down to three questions: how much you can spend per seat, how soon people need to sit down, and how the space needs to look. The good news is that you do not have to pick one answer for the whole office. Most smart buyers mix tiers by room. This guide compares the three tiers side by side with real prices and lead times, then walks through the checks that keep a purchase from going sideways.

The three tiers, explained

Liquidation-grade furniture comes out of corporate offices that are closing, downsizing or relocating. Each pre-owned piece carries a condition grade, so you know what you are getting. It is the fastest and least expensive way to get a working seat.

Remanufactured furniture starts as pre-owned product and is refurbished before it ships. Expect a more consistent look across a group of stations than you would get from liquidation stock, at roughly double the liquidation price.

New furniture is built to your specification. You get full choice of finishes and configuration, and you pay for it in both price and waiting time.

What a real Arizona budget looks like

Furniture price is only part of the number. For offices in Greater Phoenix, add these line items:

  • Delivery: $195 for central zones or $295 for the wider metro. Outside Greater Phoenix, including Tucson and Flagstaff, freight is quoted per project.
  • Installation: $95 per workstation, with a $495 minimum.
  • Electrical: not included. Powered panels still need an electrician to connect to the building.
  • Volume pricing: 5% off at 20 or more stations, 8% at 50 or more, and 12% at 100 or more.

At 10 stations no volume discount applies. At 20 stations the 5% tier starts, so it can be worth checking whether a few more seats you already plan to add push you over the line.

Mix tiers by room. You do not need one tier for the whole floor. A common pattern is remanufactured or new in the areas clients see, and liquidation-grade in training rooms, call areas and overflow seating. Free space planning makes it easy to price both side by side.

Brands, grades and warranties

Pre-owned inventory can include Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth, Humanscale, Teknion, Allsteel, Kimball, AIS and Friant. Office Station sells these brands as an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, so factor that into how you compare a pre-owned designer chair with a new one.

Condition grades tell you what to expect:

  • Grade A: like new.
  • Grade B: very good.
  • Grade C: good.

If you are shopping seating specifically, see pre-owned office chairs. For desks, tables and storage, browse pre-owned office furniture.

Arizona buyer checklist

  1. Measure the space and get a plan. Confirm station count, aisle widths and where power and data will feed in.
  2. Set your move-in date first. If you need seats in under two weeks, new furniture is likely off the table.
  3. Decide tier by room, not for the whole office.
  4. Ask for the condition grade on every pre-owned line item.
  5. Book your electrician early. Electrical is outside the furniture price and can become the bottleneck.
  6. Confirm building logistics. Loading dock hours, freight elevator reservations and any insurance certificate your landlord requires.
  7. Price the whole job: furniture, delivery zone, installation and volume discount together.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to furnish an office in Arizona?

Liquidation-grade furniture is the lowest-cost tier. At Office Station, a 6'x6' workstation, furniture only, is $615 liquidation-grade compared with $1,245 remanufactured and $1,920 new. Electrical, delivery and professional installation are priced separately.

How fast can I get office furniture in Phoenix?

Liquidation-grade workstations can be available in stock in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured stations usually take 7 to 14 days, and new stations take 4 to 6 weeks. Greater Phoenix delivery is $195 for central zones and $295 for the wider metro.

Does pre-owned designer furniture come with a manufacturer warranty?

Generally, no. Office Station sells pre-owned Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll and other brands as an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer, and manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Ask about condition grade and inspect the specific pieces before you buy.

What do the condition grades A, B and C mean?

Grade A is like new, Grade B is very good and Grade C is good. The grade describes cosmetic and functional condition. For client-facing areas many buyers choose A or B, while back-office and training rooms are common places to save with C.

Do you charge for space planning?

No. Space planning is free. It helps you confirm how many stations fit, where power needs to land and which configuration to quote before you commit.
