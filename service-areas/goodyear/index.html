How your net offer is calculated

The written offer equals resale value minus removal cost. Resale value depends on brand, condition and matching quantities. Removal cost depends on labor, access and disposal. When resale exceeds removal, Office Station pays you the difference.

Rushed schedules can raise removal cost, since larger crews or after-hours work may be needed. That is another reason to request the walkthrough early, even if your move date feels far away.

Speed tradeoffs worth knowing

Moving fast is possible, but it helps to know where speed costs something and where it does not.

Where speed is free

  • Requesting the walkthrough early. There is no downside to getting the itemized inventory and offer in hand weeks ahead of your move.
  • Sending counts in advance. A rough list of workstations, chairs and storage lets the walkthrough focus on condition and access.
  • Clearing personal items first. Empty drawers and bare work surfaces make disassembly quicker for the crew.

Where speed can cost you

  • Last-minute removal dates. A compressed window may need more crew hours, which raises removal cost and lowers the net.
  • Waiting to order workstations. Liquidation-grade stock moves. If the configuration you want sells, your next option may be remanufactured at a longer lead time.
  • Skipping space planning. Ordering before confirming fit can lead to extra stations or a layout that fights your electrical locations.

For most Goodyear offices, the best approach is to run both tracks in parallel: request the liquidation walkthrough for the old space and a cubicle quote for the new one on the same day. That way the removal date and the delivery date can be set together, and your team is not left without desks between the two.

What to prepare this week

  1. Email your property manager for COI requirements and move-hour restrictions.
  2. Count workstations, chairs, conference tables and storage.
  3. Flag computers and drives that need certified data destruction.
  4. Pick your target handback date and your new-space opening date.
  5. Request the walkthrough and a cubicle quote at the same time.
