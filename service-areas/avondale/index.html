Liquidating an Avondale office

Every liquidation starts with a walkthrough. In Greater Phoenix, including Avondale, that typically happens within 48 hours of your request. We note brands, quantities, condition and the access details that affect removal, such as dock availability and whether crews need to work after your staff leave.

Within about 5 business days you receive a written net offer. It is simple math: the resale value of the furniture minus the cost to remove it. A positive result means you are paid. A negative result means the offer states what the cleanout costs, so you can budget with a firm number instead of an estimate from a hauler.

What is included

  • Itemized inventory of everything being removed
  • Certificates of insurance for your landlord or property manager
  • Nights and weekends crews when the building requires them
  • E-waste handling with certificates of data destruction
  • A photo-documented broom-clean handback and a diversion summary

What makes an Avondale inventory worth more

West Valley offices hold a wide mix of furniture, from branded systems installed at build-out to desks bought piecemeal over the years. The offer reflects that mix item by item. A few things consistently help the resale side of the math:

  • Branded systems furniture. Workstations and seating from makers like Steelcase, Herman Miller, Haworth, Knoll or Allsteel are easier to resell than unbranded pieces.
  • Runs of matching stations. A block of identical workstations is far more marketable than scattered one-off desks.
  • Complete parts. Keep connectors, hardware, pedestals and keys with the furniture rather than tossing them during packing.
  • Clean, working chairs. Task chairs with intact mechanisms and upholstery hold value; broken ones mostly add disposal cost.

On the cost side, the biggest variables are access and timing. A suite with a nearby dock and flexible hours is quicker to clear than one that requires long carries during a narrow after-hours window. If your building allows it, sharing a floor plan and the move rules before the walkthrough helps the offer land closer to the final number.

Selling some, keeping some

You do not have to liquidate everything. Because the inventory is itemized, you can see which pieces add value and which only add removal cost, then decide what to bring to a new location. Many Avondale teams keep their best chairs and sell the systems furniture that will not fit a new floor plan.
