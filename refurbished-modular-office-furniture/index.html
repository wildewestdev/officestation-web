Refurbished Modular Office Furniture

Remanufactured modular workstations run $1,245 per 6'x6' station and usually ship in 7-14 days. Here is how that option compares with liquidation-grade and new, and what to check before you buy.

6 minute read

Modular workstations are the backbone of most open offices. They are also one of the biggest line items on a build-out budget. Refurbished modular office furniture gives you a middle path: systems built by the major manufacturers, restored to a consistent standard, at a price well below new and on a timeline measured in days rather than weeks.

This page walks through what you actually get with a remanufactured workstation, how the numbers compare, and what to check before you sign off on an order.

Why remanufactured is often the sweet spot

A remanufactured station costs $675 less than new per 6'x6' workstation, about 35% below the new price. On a 40-station floor, that is $27,000 in furniture savings before any volume discount.

Compared with liquidation-grade, you pay $630 more per station. In return, you get a more uniform appearance across the floor, which matters when clients walk through or when you are filling a large open area where mismatched panels would stand out.

The lead time is the other lever. At 7-14 days, remanufactured furniture fits most lease-start dates that would be missed by a 4-6 week new order. If your move date is fixed and your landlord is not flexible, that gap can be the deciding factor.

Plan the budget in full. Workstation prices cover furniture only. Add power and data from your electrician, delivery ($195 central or $295 metro in Greater Phoenix, freight quoted per project elsewhere) and installation at $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum.

What to check before you buy

Use this checklist when you review a quote or walk inventory in person.

  • Scope of work. Get a written description of what was replaced or refinished: fabric, worksurface laminate, edge banding, hardware, keys and locks.
  • Condition grade. Office Station grades pre-owned furniture A (like new), B (very good) or C (good). Confirm the grade applies to every component, not just the panels.
  • Panel heights and finishes. Make sure all stations in one area share the same panel height and fabric so the floor reads as one system.
  • Power and data. Confirm whether panels are powered, what the base feed is, and whether your electrician can connect to it. Electrical is not included in furniture pricing.
  • Storage and pedestals. Count pedestals, overheads and lateral files per station. Missing storage is the most common gap between a quote and a finished floor.
  • Keys. Ask for keyed-alike or master-keyed locks if security matters.
  • Future expansion. Ask whether matching components are likely to be available if you add stations later. Pre-owned inventory changes, so buy a few spare parts now if growth is likely.

Mixing tiers on one project

You do not have to choose one tier for the whole office. A common approach is remanufactured stations on the main floor where appearance matters, and liquidation-grade stations in back-office, training or overflow areas where cost and speed matter more.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between refurbished and remanufactured modular furniture?

The terms get used loosely across the industry. Refurbished can mean anything from a cleaning to new fabric, while remanufactured usually implies components were taken down, inspected and rebuilt with replaced wear parts and finishes. Always ask for a written description of what was done to the panels, worksurfaces and storage before you compare quotes.

How much does a remanufactured 6x6 workstation cost?

Office Station prices a remanufactured 6'x6' workstation at $1,245, furniture only. A liquidation-grade station is $615 and a new station is $1,920. Prices exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation.

How long does it take to get remanufactured workstations?

Remanufactured stations usually take 7-14 days. Liquidation-grade stations in stock can be ready in as little as 72 hours, and new furniture typically runs 4-6 weeks.

Does the manufacturer warranty come with refurbished furniture?

Generally no. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer, and manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Ask about condition grades and inspect finishes, hardware and cable management before you commit.

Are there discounts for larger orders?

Yes. Volume pricing is 5% off at 20 or more stations, 8% off at 50 or more, and 12% off at 100 or more. Space planning is free, and installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum.
