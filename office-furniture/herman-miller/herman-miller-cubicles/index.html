Used Herman Miller Cubicles

Herman Miller panel systems were built to be taken apart and rebuilt, which makes them some of the most practical used cubicles you can buy. Here is how the lines differ and what each pricing tier gets you.

6 minute read

A cubicle is really a kit of parts: panels, connectors, worksurfaces, supports, storage and power. Herman Miller designed its systems so those parts can be disassembled at one office and reassembled into a different footprint at another. That is the core reason used Herman Miller cubicles work so well. You are not stuck with the last tenant's layout.

This hub helps you compare the lines you are likely to encounter, understand the three pricing tiers, and avoid the mistakes that make a used cubicle project cost more than it should.

Herman Miller workstation lines on the used market

Several Herman Miller systems circulate in the secondary market. Availability shifts with what comes out of corporate liquidations, so treat this as a map, not an inventory list.

Action Office

A long-running panel system that was widely installed in corporate offices. Because it was in production for so long, parts and panels are often easier to find, which makes it a solid choice for expanding or repairing an existing layout.

Ethospace

A frame-and-tile system. Tiles can be swapped, for example fabric, glass or markerboard, so you can change the look and privacy of a station without replacing the frame. It tends to read as more architectural than a basic panel system.

Canvas and other newer systems

Newer Herman Miller workstation families lean toward lower, more open benching and desk-based layouts. These suit teams that want collaboration and daylight over high privacy walls.

Stay within one line per area. Panel connectors, power harnesses and worksurface brackets are generally specific to a system. Mixing lines in the same cluster often means missing parts and a slow install. If you are adding to an existing space, tell us the line you already have.

Which tier fits your project?

  • Choose liquidation-grade when speed and budget lead, the space is not client-facing, or you are expanding with a quick-turn team.
  • Choose remanufactured when you want used economics but a cohesive, refreshed look across the floor.
  • Choose new when you need a specific current finish or configuration and can wait 4 to 6 weeks.

Condition grades apply within the pre-owned tiers: A, like new; B, very good; and C, good.

Checklist before you commit

  1. Measure the space, including column locations, door swings and aisle widths.
  2. Choose panel heights by team: lower for collaboration, taller for focus or phone-heavy roles.
  3. Confirm worksurface sizes and whether each station includes storage.
  4. Identify which power components are included and book a licensed electrician early.
  5. Match fabric and finish across the full quantity, or plan zones intentionally.
  6. Order a few spare connectors and panels for future changes.
  7. Coordinate building access, freight elevator and dock times for delivery day.
  8. Remember that manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture.

Reconfiguring a used system for your floor

The layout a system came out of rarely matches the space it is going into. That is normal. A pre-owned cluster of eight 8'x8' stations can often be rebuilt as a larger number of smaller stations, or opened up into lower-panel pods, as long as the right connectors and worksurfaces are available.

The practical limits are parts, not panels. Corner and three-way connectors, finished end panels and matching worksurfaces run out before straight panels do. Share your floor plan early so the plan is built around parts that actually exist, rather than a drawing that stalls on install day.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a used Herman Miller cubicle cost?

As a benchmark, a 6'x6' workstation is $615 liquidation-grade or $1,245 remanufactured, furniture only, compared with $1,920 new. Prices exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation. Final pricing depends on the line, panel heights, worksurfaces and storage you choose.

Can used Herman Miller panels be mixed with other brands?

Usually not in a clean way. Panel connections, power components and worksurface brackets are generally specific to a product line. Plan an area around one line so every part fits together, and treat mixing as something to verify before you buy.

What is the difference between liquidation-grade and remanufactured?

Liquidation-grade workstations are cleaned and sold in their existing finishes and fabrics, and in-stock items can ship in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured workstations are refreshed, for example with new fabric or surfaces, and usually take 7 to 14 days.

Do used Herman Miller cubicles include electrical?

Our workstation prices exclude electrical. Some panel systems include power components, but connecting them to your building requires a licensed electrician and should be planned early. Confirm which power parts are included in any quote.

Is there a warranty on used Herman Miller workstations?

Generally, no manufacturer warranty transfers. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized Herman Miller dealer. Each item carries a condition grade of A, B or C so you know what you are buying.
