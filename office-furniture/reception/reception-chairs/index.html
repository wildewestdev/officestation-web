Used Reception Chairs

Guests judge your business from the chair they wait in. Used reception chairs from designer brands give your lobby a polished look, with grading you can trust and delivery handled in one quote.

6 minute read

Reception chairs do a quiet but important job. They are the first place a client, candidate or patient sits, and they set the tone before anyone says hello. Used reception chairs from established commercial brands let you create a lobby that looks considered and holds up to daily traffic, at a price that makes sense for a room people spend a few minutes in.

Office Station sells pre-owned lobby seating recovered from corporate office liquidations. Each chair carries a condition grade, and delivery and setup are quoted together with your order so there are no surprises on install day.

Condition grades for visible seating

Every guest sees your lobby chairs up close, so grade is worth thinking about.

  • Grade A, like new. Minimal to no visible wear. The right call for primary lobby seating in client-facing businesses.
  • Grade B, very good. Light, normal signs of use. Works well for secondary seating, break rooms that double as waiting space, and interior corridors.
  • Grade C, good. Visible cosmetic wear with sound construction. Better suited to employee entrances, training rooms and staff areas than your front lobby.

What to check before you buy

Reception chairs wear differently than task chairs. There are no gas cylinders or tilt mechanisms, so focus on these points:

  1. Seat front edge. This is where upholstery wears first. Look for thinning, shine or splitting.
  2. Seams and piping. Check for loose stitching, especially on vinyl and leather.
  3. Arm caps and arm fronts. Hands rest here all day. Look for scratches, worn finish or loose caps.
  4. Frame joints. Sit, lean and shift. A good chair stays silent and solid.
  5. Glides and feet. Missing or worn glides scratch floors and make chairs rock.
  6. Cleanability. In medical or food-adjacent settings, confirm the upholstery can handle your cleaning routine.
  7. Matching. If you need a set, confirm color, finish and upholstery match across every chair.
Buying for a medical or dental lobby? Choose chairs with arms so guests can sit and stand easily, add a few seats with a slightly higher seat height, and favor wipeable upholstery. Ask us about seat height and upholstery type on any chair you are considering.

Frequently asked questions

What styles of used reception chairs are available?

Pre-owned lobby seating commonly includes lounge chairs, guest side chairs with or without arms, benches and modular or beam seating. What is available at any time depends on current office liquidation projects. Tell us your style, count and color range and we will tell you what we have.

Which upholstery is best for a busy lobby?

Vinyl, polyurethane and leather wipe clean easily and suit medical and high-traffic lobbies. Commercial fabric feels warmer and hides minor wear well. Whatever you choose, look at the condition of seams and seat fronts, which wear first.

Can delivery and setup be included in one quote?

Yes. We quote delivery and any setup together with your chairs so you have one number. In Greater Phoenix, delivery is $195 central and $295 metro, and freight elsewhere is quoted per project.

Do used reception chairs have a warranty?

Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer for any brand. Each chair has an A, B or C grade so you know its condition before you buy.

How many reception chairs do I need?

Count the most visitors you expect waiting at the same time, then add one or two. Busy offices with appointment overlap, such as medical and legal practices, often need more seating than the average day suggests.
