Used Mobile Office Storage

Rolling pedestals, carts and personal caddies let your layout change without calling a crew. Every pre-owned piece is graded so you can buy with confidence.

5 minute read

Offices do not hold still anymore. Teams grow and shrink, departments shift floors, and a room that was a training space last quarter is a project hub this quarter. Used mobile office storage gives you storage that moves with those changes, without a crew, a drill or a reconfiguration budget.

Office Station offers pre-owned mobile storage pulled from corporate spaces and graded A, B or C. This page focuses on how to choose rolling storage for a flexible layout and what to check before you buy.

When mobile storage makes sense

Fixed storage is the right answer when a desk will never move. Mobile storage earns its place when flexibility is the point.

  • Hot desking and hoteling. A personal caddy lets someone roll their belongings to whichever desk they use that day.
  • Benching and open plans. Rolling pedestals slide out of the way when rows get rearranged.
  • Collaboration rooms. Carts bring supplies, markers and devices to the table, then park in a corner.
  • Height-adjustable desks. A freestanding mobile unit does not fight the desk as it rises and lowers.
  • Phased moves. Storage can travel with a team during a corporate relocation without being disassembled.

What to check on used mobile storage

Rolling units take abuse that fixed storage never sees. They get pushed over thresholds, bumped into walls and loaded unevenly. Look closely at these points.

  1. Casters. Roll the unit in every direction. Each caster should spin and swivel freely with no flat spots.
  2. Caster locks. If present, confirm they actually hold the unit in place on your floor type.
  3. Stability. Open the top drawer when the unit is loaded. It should not tip forward. Counterweighted units help here.
  4. Drawer slides. Check for smooth travel and full closure.
  5. Lock and key. Test the lock and ask about keys or a replaceable core.
  6. Fit. Measure under-desk clearance and depth against the unit, including caster height.
  7. Floor type. Hard casters suit carpet, softer casters suit hard floors. Match them to your surface.

Condition grades

Every pre-owned unit is graded so you know what to expect.

  • Grade A, like new. Minimal or no visible wear, casters and slides in excellent shape.
  • Grade B, very good. Light marks from normal use, fully functional.
  • Grade C, good. Visible scuffs or scratches, still rolls and operates as intended.

How much mobile storage do you need

Start with how people work, not with how many desks you have. A team where everyone has an assigned seat usually needs one pedestal per person. A shared-desk program works differently, because storage follows the person rather than the desk.

  • Assigned seating. Plan one pedestal per workstation, sized to that person's paper and supply needs.
  • Shared desks. Plan one personal caddy per person who uses the space, plus a parking area sized for all of them at once.
  • Team zones. One or two supply carts per zone is usually enough, placed where they will not block traffic.
  • Meeting rooms. A single cart per room keeps markers, adapters and cleaning supplies close.

Before you order, walk the floor with a tape measure and mark where each unit will park at the end of the day. If you cannot find a clear spot, reduce the count or choose a slimmer unit. It is easier to add a few pieces later than to live with crowded aisles.

Mobile storage in shared zones takes more bumps over time, so many buyers choose Grade B or C for high-traffic areas and save Grade A for private offices. Frequently asked questions

What counts as mobile office storage?

Mobile storage is any storage unit on casters that a person can move without tools. The most common types are rolling pedestals that tuck under a desk, personal storage caddies used in shared or hot-desk areas, and utility carts for supplies or equipment. They are popular in offices that reconfigure often.

Will a used mobile pedestal fit under my desk?

Measure the clearance under your work surface, including any modesty panel, keyboard tray or support beam. The pedestal height plus casters must be less than that clearance. Also check the depth so the unit does not stick out past the front edge of the desk.

Are the casters on used mobile storage replaceable?

Often, yes. Many units use standard stem casters that can be swapped if one is worn or flat-spotted. Still, check that every caster rolls and swivels freely before buying, and use locking casters on units that will stay put most of the time.

Is mobile storage secure enough for personal items?

Most mobile pedestals and caddies include a lock, but keys are not always available on pre-owned units. Ask about key status, and look for a replaceable lock core if the key is missing. For shared spaces, consider a lock plan before rollout.

How fast can I get used mobile storage?

In-stock liquidation-grade units can be ready in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured pieces usually take 7 to 14 days, and new furniture typically takes 4 to 6 weeks.
