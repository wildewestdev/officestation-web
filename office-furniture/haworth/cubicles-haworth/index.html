Used Haworth Cubicles

Haworth panel systems are built to be taken down, moved and rebuilt, which makes them one of the better candidates for a second life. Here is how to compare lines, condition tiers and real costs before you buy.

6 minute read

Why used Haworth cubicles hold up

Haworth has made panel-based workstations for decades, and most of its systems were designed for reconfiguration. Panels connect with hardware that can be released and reattached, work surfaces hang on standards or brackets, and storage is modular. That design intent is exactly what you want in pre-owned furniture: a system that was meant to be moved survives being moved.

When you buy used Haworth cubicles, you are not really buying a single product. You are buying a panel line, a generation within that line, a set of finishes and a condition level. Getting those four things right matters more than the brand name on the label.

Common Haworth panel lines you will see

Pre-owned inventory tends to come from office decommissioning projects, so what is available reflects what companies bought years ago. These are the Haworth families that show up most often in the used market.

Unigroup

A long-running monolithic panel system and one of the most widely installed Haworth lines. It is a practical choice for expanding an existing Unigroup floor, because parts are common. Check that panel heights and connector styles match what you already own.

Premise

A panel system often used for clean, straightforward workstation runs. Premise frequently appears in liquidation-grade inventory and can be a budget-friendly way to outfit a large open area.

Compose

A frame-and-tile style system that lets you mix fabric, laminate and glass tiles on the same panel. Compose is a good fit when you want a more current look or plan to change tile finishes later without replacing frames.

Patterns and Places

Lighter, more open planning systems built around desking and lower screens. These suit collaborative layouts where full-height privacy panels are not the goal.

Match the line before you match the color. A matching fabric from a different Haworth line will look right in photos and still fail to connect. Always confirm the product family and generation first, then finishes.

What to check on used Haworth cubicles

Whether you buy from us or anyone else, walk through this list before you sign.

  1. Product line and generation. Ask for the family name and, if you are adding to an existing floor, compare connector hardware side by side.
  2. Panel heights. Mixed heights are fine by design, but make sure you are getting the heights your layout calls for.
  3. Complete connector kits. Missing corner posts, end caps or connectors are the most common cause of install delays.
  4. Fabric condition. Look for fading along window walls, stains and snags. Remanufactured tiers address this with new fabric.
  5. Work surface edges and supports. Check for chipped edges, delamination and bent brackets.
  6. Power and data components. Confirm which base feeds and harnesses are included and plan for an electrician to connect them.
  7. Storage keys. Pedestals and overheads should come with keys or be rekeyable.

Frequently asked questions

Is Office Station an authorized Haworth dealer?

No. Office Station is an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture, including Haworth. We are not affiliated with or authorized by the manufacturer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture.

How much does a used Haworth cubicle cost?

For a 6'x6' workstation, furniture only, liquidation-grade runs $615, remanufactured runs $1,245 and new runs $1,920. Those prices exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation. Volume discounts start at 20 stations.

Can I add used Haworth panels to my existing Haworth workstations?

Often, yes, if the new pieces come from the same panel line and generation. Connectors, panel heights and powering components vary by line, so share the product name, photos of the connectors and your panel heights before ordering. Mixing different lines usually does not work without adapters or a full swap.

How fast can I get used Haworth cubicles?

Liquidation-grade stations that are in stock can ship in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured stations usually take 7 to 14 days, and new stations take 4 to 6 weeks.
