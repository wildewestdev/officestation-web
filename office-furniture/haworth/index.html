Used Haworth Office Furniture

Haworth builds furniture meant to be reconfigured, which makes it a strong candidate for a second life. Here is how to buy pre-owned Haworth with clear eyes about condition, compatibility and warranty.

6 minute read

Haworth is one of the major names in commercial office furniture, known for workstation systems, seating, desking and storage built for large corporate floors. A lot of that furniture comes out of offices long before it wears out, often because a company moved, downsized or changed its layout. That is where pre-owned Haworth comes in.

Warranty transparency

Warranty questions come up on every pre-owned purchase, so here is the answer plainly.

No manufacturer warranty assumed. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized Haworth dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Please do not buy used Haworth expecting the manufacturer to cover it. Buy it for its build quality and price, based on a condition grade and a clear description.

What that means in practice:

  • Inspect or ask before ordering. Use the checklists below and ask for photos or details on anything that matters to you.
  • Plan for spares. On larger workstation orders, a few extra connectors, panels or worksurfaces can save a scramble later.
  • Keep your records. Note the product line and any labels, which helps if you need to source matching pieces down the road.

Condition grades

  • Grade A, like new. Minimal to no visible wear.
  • Grade B, very good. Light cosmetic wear, fully functional.
  • Grade C, good. Noticeable cosmetic wear, fully usable, lowest price.

Matching used Haworth to what you already own

A common reason to buy used Haworth is to expand an existing Haworth installation. It can work well, but systems furniture is particular.

  1. Identify the line. Look for labels under worksurfaces or inside panel frames, and photograph connectors and power components.
  2. Match the generation. Lines evolve over the years, and parts from different generations do not always connect.
  3. Match panel heights and widths. A mismatched height breaks the run visually and may not align with connectors.
  4. Check power. Electrified panels need compatible harnesses and base feeds. Your electrician still handles the building connection.
  5. Match finishes and fabric. Fabric colors fade and discontinue. Remanufactured panels can help unify a mixed set.

Free space planning is included, so you can confirm counts, layout and missing parts before anything ships.

When to mix brands instead

If an exact Haworth match is not available, it is sometimes smarter to keep Haworth in one zone and add a separate group of stations from another line, such as Steelcase, Knoll, Teknion or Allsteel, in its own area. Freestanding pieces like seating, height-adjustable desks, tables and storage mix across brands easily. Panel systems are the category where staying within one line pays off, because connectors and power components are rarely interchangeable.

Frequently asked questions

Is Office Station an authorized Haworth dealer?

No. Office Station is an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture, including Haworth, and is not an authorized dealer. That means pricing and availability come from the secondary market rather than from the manufacturer's dealer network.

Does the Haworth warranty transfer to used furniture?

Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Treat a used Haworth purchase as buying the product as graded and described. Ask about condition and completeness before you order so there are no surprises.

Can used Haworth panels be added to my existing Haworth workstations?

Often, if the product line and generation match. Panel systems use specific connectors, heights and power components, and parts from different lines usually do not interconnect. Share photos and any labels from your existing stations so compatibility can be checked first.

What is the difference between liquidation-grade and remanufactured?

Liquidation-grade workstations are sold essentially as removed, graded by condition, and are the lowest cost. Remanufactured workstations are refreshed before sale and cost more. For a 6'x6' workstation, furniture only, published pricing is $615 liquidation-grade and $1,245 remanufactured.

Can Office Station buy my Haworth furniture?

If your office is in one of Office Station's liquidation markets, you can request a walkthrough and a written net offer. The offer equals the resale value of the furniture minus the cost of removal, and when it is positive, you are paid. Quality brand furniture in good condition generally helps that number.
