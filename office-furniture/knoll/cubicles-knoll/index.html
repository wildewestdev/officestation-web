Used Knoll Cubicles: Three Tiers, Clear Pricing

Knoll workstation pricing depends mostly on which tier you choose. Compare liquidation-grade, remanufactured and new side by side, then add installation and delivery to see a realistic total.

6 minute read

Knoll systems furniture shows up in offices of every size, and it is built to be reconfigured and reused. That makes used Knoll cubicles a strong option when you need workstations without paying full new-furniture prices.

The biggest lever on your cost is not the brand. It is the tier. This page lays out our three tiers, runs the math on a sample project, and lists the costs that sit outside the furniture price so nothing surprises you.

How to choose a tier

Choose liquidation-grade when time or budget rules

If you need people seated this week, liquidation-grade is the practical answer. You are buying pre-owned workstations in their graded condition: A like new, B very good, or C good. For many teams, a Grade B workstation is indistinguishable in daily use from something far more expensive.

Choose remanufactured when appearance matters

Remanufactured sits in the middle. It costs roughly double liquidation-grade but still about a third less than new, and it usually lands in 7 to 14 days. It suits offices with client traffic or a leadership team that wants the space to feel fresh.

Choose new when specifications are fixed

If your design calls for exact current finishes or a manufacturer-new product, new is the right tier. Build the 4 to 6 week lead time into your move schedule.

You can mix tiers. Many layouts put remanufactured stations in visible areas and liquidation-grade stations in back-of-house zones. As long as the components are compatible, mixing tiers is one of the most effective ways to cut a workstation budget.

Costs outside the furniture price

  • Space planning: free. We lay out your floor before you commit.
  • Installation: $95 per workstation, $495 minimum. A five-station job, for example, would total $475 at the per-station rate, so the $495 minimum applies.
  • Delivery in Greater Phoenix: $195 for the central zone or $295 for the wider metro. See local details for Phoenix and nearby cities.
  • Delivery elsewhere: freight is quoted per project anywhere else in the country.
  • Electrical: not included. Powered panels and desking need a connection to building power, typically by a licensed electrician.

From layout to move-in

A workstation project goes smoother when the steps happen in the right order. Here is the sequence we recommend for a Knoll cubicle order:

  1. Share your floor plan and headcount. Include any columns, doors and existing power locations.
  2. Get a free space plan. We lay out workstations so you can see seat count, aisle widths and sightlines before you spend anything.
  3. Pick a tier, or a mix. Match the tier to your deadline and budget using the comparison above.
  4. Book your electrician. Give them the plan early so power is ready when furniture arrives.
  5. Confirm building logistics. Freight elevator reservations, dock times and certificate of insurance requirements belong on the calendar before install day.
  6. Install and walk the floor. Check every station for level worksurfaces, secure panels and working drawers before your team moves in.

If you are on a tight deadline, tell us your move-in date first. That single detail usually decides the tier.

Knoll-specific checklist before you order

  1. Confirm the product line. Knoll systems such as Dividends Horizon, Reff and Morrison use different components, and parts are not interchangeable across lines.
  2. Match panel or screen heights to the privacy and sightlines you want.
  3. If you are adding to existing Knoll workstations, send photos of connectors and worksurface brackets.
  4. Decide on storage per station: pedestals, overhead bins or none.
  5. Map power and data locations so your electrician can plan early.
  6. Check freight elevator, dock and building delivery rules for install day.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a used Knoll cubicle cost?

For a 6'x6' workstation, furniture only, liquidation-grade is $615, remanufactured is $1,245 and new is $1,920. Those prices exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation. Other sizes and configurations are quoted.

What is the difference between liquidation-grade and remanufactured?

Liquidation-grade workstations are pre-owned and sold in their graded condition, which makes them the lowest cost and fastest option. Remanufactured workstations go through additional reconditioning before sale, which costs more and usually takes 7 to 14 days. New workstations typically take 4 to 6 weeks.

Do you install Knoll cubicles?

Yes. Professional installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum. Space planning is free. Electrical connection to building power is not included, so plan for a licensed electrician where your layout requires it.

Are there discounts for large orders?

Yes. Volume pricing is 5% at 20 or more stations, 8% at 50 or more, and 12% at 100 or more. Use the quote builder to see how volume changes your total.

Do Knoll warranties cover used cubicles?

Generally, no. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized Knoll dealer, and manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Ask us what terms apply to your order before you buy.
