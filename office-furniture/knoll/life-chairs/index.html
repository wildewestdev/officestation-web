Used Knoll Life Chairs

A used Knoll Life chair can be a great buy, as long as you know what you are getting. This page covers grades, warranty realities, inspection points and how quickly you can have chairs in place.

6 minute read

The Knoll Life chair was designed around a simple idea: a task chair should be light, uncluttered and comfortable without a wall of levers. That makes a used Knoll Life chair appealing for offices that want recognizable design and real ergonomics without new-chair pricing. It also means you should buy with clear eyes, because a pre-owned chair is only as good as its condition and the honesty of the seller.

Office Station is an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture. We are not an authorized Knoll dealer. What we can give you is a clear grade, straight answers on warranty and fast availability on chairs that are already in stock.

The honest truth about warranty

This is the question most buyers ask first, so we will answer it up front.

Assume no manufacturer warranty. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. When you buy a used Life chair from any independent reseller, including us, plan as if the original Knoll warranty does not apply. Your protection comes from the condition grade, a careful inspection and a seller who tells you what they know.

That may sound like a reason to hesitate. In practice, a well-built commercial chair that has already survived years of office use, and still passes inspection, has proven itself. The parts most likely to wear, such as gas cylinders, casters and arm pads, are also the parts easiest to spot when you check a chair carefully.

So the smart approach is not to avoid used chairs. It is to know what to look for and to buy from a source that grades honestly.

How we grade Life chairs

Grades describe cosmetic condition. Any chair we list should work as intended.

  • Grade A, like new. Minimal to no visible wear. Ideal for executive offices and client-facing areas.
  • Grade B, very good. Light, normal signs of use like minor base scuffs or slight pad wear. The everyday value choice.
  • Grade C, good. More visible cosmetic wear with working mechanisms. A practical pick for training rooms and budget-driven rollouts.

Fast availability, and what drives it

Speed is one of the biggest advantages of pre-owned seating. New commercial furniture often takes 4 to 6 weeks. In-stock pre-owned items can be ready in as little as 72 hours. If a remanufactured item is involved, plan on about 7 to 14 days.

What you should know about supply:

  • Inventory comes from real projects. Our chairs come out of corporate office liquidations and decommissioning work, so availability changes as projects complete.
  • Small quantities move fastest. A few matching chairs are usually easier to fill than a large matched set.
  • Flexibility helps. If you can accept a couple of colors or mix Grade A and B, your order can often be filled sooner.

Is the Life chair right for you?

The Life chair is a good fit when:

  • Users want a lighter, visually simple task chair.
  • Most people work at a desk for part of the day, not locked in for ten hours straight.
  • You value clean design in shared spaces and meeting rooms.

Consider something else when users want heavy-duty, highly adjustable support or a back that flexes as they move. Knoll's Generation chair is built for that style of sitting; see our used Knoll Generation chairs page. Frequently asked questions

Do used Knoll Life chairs come with a manufacturer warranty?

Generally, no. Manufacturer warranties typically do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, and Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized Knoll dealer. Buy based on the condition grade and your own inspection, and ask us any questions before you order.

How quickly can I get a used Life chair?

In-stock pre-owned items can be ready in as little as 72 hours. Greater Phoenix delivery is $195 central and $295 metro. Freight to other locations is quoted per project.

What is the difference between Grade A, B and C?

Grade A is like new with minimal visible wear. Grade B is very good with light, normal signs of use. Grade C is good, with more visible cosmetic wear but working mechanisms.

Is a Life chair or a Generation chair better for my team?

It depends on how people sit. Life suits users who like a lighter, more minimal task chair with straightforward adjustments. Generation suits users who move and shift a lot and want a back that flexes with them.

Can you tell me the exact configuration before I buy?

Yes. Ask about the specific chairs you are considering and we will share what we know about adjustments, upholstery and condition. If a detail is unknown, we will say so rather than guess.
