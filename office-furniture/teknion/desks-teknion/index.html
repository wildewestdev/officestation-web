Used Teknion Desks

A pre-owned Teknion desk can give you corporate-grade build quality without new-furniture wait times. Here is how grading, lead times and delivery work so you can plan with confidence.

6 minute read

What to expect from a used Teknion desk

Teknion desks usually come out of corporate offices, where they were specified as part of a coordinated furniture program. That means you often find them in matched sets: private office suites, height-adjustable tables, benching desks and the storage that goes with them.

The appeal is simple. You get solid frames, commercial worksurfaces and a clean corporate look, and you can have it far sooner than a new order. The tradeoff is that you buy what is available, in the finish it comes in, graded honestly.

Office Station sells pre-owned Teknion as an independent reseller. We are not an authorized Teknion dealer, and manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned pieces. Our grading is how we make condition clear up front.

Common types of Teknion desks on the pre-owned market

Private office desks and suites

A desk, return or credenza, and often an overhead or bookcase. These suit managers, attorneys and anyone who meets visitors at their desk. Check that every piece in the suite shares the same finish, because suites sometimes get split up over the years.

Height-adjustable desks

Sit-stand tables are among the most requested pre-owned pieces. Test the full range of travel, listen for a smooth motor, and confirm any keypad or controller works. Ask whether the power cord and controller are included.

Benching and shared desks

Long runs of shared worksurfaces with a common spine. Great for open teams and hot-desking. The key check is that you get every leg, beam and connector for the run you plan.

Tables and meeting desks

Small conference and touchdown tables round out a floor. Look for flat tops and sound edge banding.

A mixed-grade order is often the smartest buy. Put Grade A in the spaces visitors see, and use Grade B or C where function matters more than looks.

Grade the parts you touch, not just the top

A worksurface can look perfect in a photo while the parts you use every day tell a different story. When you review a specific desk, look at drawer slides, lock cylinders, leveling glides and the brackets that hold a return to the main top. Loose or stripped fasteners are usually a simple fix, but you want to know about them before installation day rather than after.

For sit-stand desks, ask whether both legs rise evenly and whether the desk holds its height under a normal load. A slight wobble at full height is common on many designs, while a desk that drifts down or stalls partway is a sign to pass or ask for a different unit.

Lead times: pick the tier that fits your move date

Your timeline often decides your tier more than your budget does.

  • Liquidation-grade: as little as 72 hours when the piece is in stock. Sold as graded.
  • Remanufactured: usually 7 to 14 days. Cleaned and refreshed for a more consistent look.
  • New: 4 to 6 weeks. The route to take if you need a specific size, finish or a warranty.

If you are opening an office in two weeks, liquidation-grade or remanufactured is realistic. If you have two months and a strict finish standard, new may be worth the wait.

Measure the path, not just the room. Large L-shaped tops and credenzas have to get through doors, elevators and tight corners. Before delivery day, confirm elevator dimensions, dock hours and any building rules about after-hours moves.

Pre-purchase checklist for a used Teknion desk

  1. Measure the room and mark doors, windows, outlets and data drops.
  2. Confirm the desk footprint leaves comfortable clearance behind the chair.
  3. Ask for photos of the actual piece, including edges, legs and underside brackets.
  4. For sit-stand desks, confirm the controller and power cord are included and the motor has been tested.
  5. Check that returns, credenzas and storage match the desk finish.
  6. Confirm keys for any locking drawers, or plan to rekey.
  7. Choose your grade by room: visitor-facing versus staff-only.
  8. Line up delivery access and a receiving contact at your building.

Frequently asked questions

What do the A, B and C grades mean on a used Teknion desk?

Grade A is like new with minimal visible wear. Grade B is very good, with light wear you notice only up close. Grade C is good, fully functional with visible cosmetic wear, and usually the best value for back-office or training spaces.

Does a used Teknion desk come with a Teknion warranty?

Generally no. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized Teknion dealer, and manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Check the grade and ask for photos of the specific piece before you buy.

How long does it take to get a used Teknion desk?

In-stock liquidation-grade pieces can be ready in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured pieces usually take 7 to 14 days. If you need a new desk in a specific finish or size, plan on 4 to 6 weeks.

What does delivery cost?

In Greater Phoenix, delivery is $195 for central zones and $295 for the wider metro. Outside Greater Phoenix, freight is quoted per project. Professional installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum.

Can you help me figure out how many desks fit my space?

Yes. Space planning is free. Send a floor plan or rough measurements with your headcount, and we can lay out desk sizes, aisles and storage before you commit to a purchase.
