Used Teknion Office Furniture

Teknion builds workstations, storage, tables and seating that hold up well past their first office. Here is how to buy it pre-owned with clear grades, straight warranty terms and pricing you can plan around.

6 minute read

Why pre-owned Teknion is worth a look

Teknion furniture is designed for large corporate floors, which means it is engineered for heavy daily use, repeated reconfiguration and long service lives. When a company relocates or downsizes, that furniture often has years of useful life left. Buying it pre-owned lets you put commercial-grade systems furniture in your office for a fraction of the new cost, and usually much sooner.

The trade-off is that you are buying what exists, not what you configure from a catalog. That makes grading, series matching and honest warranty expectations more important than they are with new furniture.

What pre-owned Teknion typically includes

Inventory depends on which offices are being cleared, so availability changes. These are the categories that most often come out of corporate Teknion installations:

  • Panel-based workstations. Cubicle systems with fabric or glass tiles, worksurfaces and hanging storage. These are the core of most large lots and the easiest place to save money.
  • Benching and open-plan desks. Shared-spine desking for teams that do not need high privacy panels.
  • Storage. Pedestals, lateral files, towers and credenzas. Storage is often the best-condition furniture in a liquidation because it moves the least.
  • Tables. Conference, meeting and training tables, sometimes with power modules.
  • Seating. Task, conference and guest chairs, covered in more detail below.

If you need a specific series or a matched set for a full floor, tell us the count and any existing furniture you are matching. It is easier to find the right lot when we know the target up front.

Independent reseller: what that means for you

Office Station sells pre-owned Teknion as an independent reseller. We are not an authorized Teknion dealer and are not affiliated with the manufacturer. In practice, that means three things:

  • No transferred warranty. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture.
  • No factory ordering. We cannot order new Teknion parts or finishes through the manufacturer on your behalf.
  • Condition is the promise. Every item is graded so you know what you are getting before it arrives.
Read the grade, then inspect the moving parts. A condition grade describes appearance and overall state. For chairs, pedestals and height-adjustable tables, also test the mechanisms, locks and cylinders, because that is where wear shows up first on any brand.

Liquidation-grade vs remanufactured pricing

Pre-owned systems furniture comes in two broad forms. Liquidation-grade furniture is sold largely as it came out of the previous office, cleaned and graded. Remanufactured furniture has been reworked, which can include new fabric, refinished surfaces or replaced components, so it looks more consistent across a large floor.

What to check before buying used Teknion

  1. Series match. If you are expanding an existing Teknion floor, confirm the product line. Components from different lines usually do not connect.
  2. Panel and frame condition. Look for bent frames, missing connectors and stained or torn fabric tiles.
  3. Worksurfaces. Check edges, brackets and any grommet holes against where your power and data will come in.
  4. Storage keys and locks. Confirm pedestals and overheads either come with keys or can be rekeyed.
  5. Power components. Powered panel components should be evaluated by a licensed electrician before connection. Electrical is not included in furniture pricing.
  6. Quantities and finish consistency. For a full floor, confirm the lot is large enough and finishes match across every station.

Frequently asked questions

Is Office Station an authorized Teknion dealer?

No. Office Station is an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture and is not an authorized Teknion dealer. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Teknion. Teknion is a trademark of its owner and is used here only to describe the furniture we resell.

Does the Teknion warranty transfer to used furniture?

Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Plan on buying based on condition grade and inspection rather than a factory warranty. Ask us about the specific items you are considering before you order.

How much does a used Teknion workstation cost?

As a planning benchmark, a 6x6 workstation, furniture only, runs about $615 liquidation-grade, $1,245 remanufactured, and $1,920 new. Actual Teknion pricing depends on series, components and grade. Prices exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation.

Can I add used Teknion to an existing Teknion floor?

Often, but match the series first. Panels, connectors, worksurface brackets and finishes vary between product lines and production years. Send photos and part labels from your existing stations so we can check compatibility before quoting.

How fast can I get used Teknion furniture?

Liquidation-grade furniture in stock can be ready in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured furniture usually takes 7 to 14 days. New furniture typically takes 4 to 6 weeks.
