Used Knoll Dividends Horizon Workstations

Dividends Horizon is a clean, low-profile panel system that holds up well on the secondary market. Here is how we grade it, how it is priced and what to check before you buy.

6 minute read

If you are shopping for used Knoll Dividends, you are most likely looking at Dividends Horizon: a steel-frame panel system known for its slim profile, segmented panel faces and tidy cable management. It is a practical choice for teams that want an open, modern look without the cost of a new order.

This page explains how pre-owned Dividends Horizon is graded, how pricing compares across tiers, and what to confirm before you buy or expand.

Why Dividends Horizon works well pre-owned

Panel systems built on a steel frame tend to survive disassembly and reassembly better than lighter systems. That makes Dividends Horizon a reasonable candidate for reuse, provided the parts are handled and inventoried carefully when they come out of a building.

  • Low visual weight. Lower panel heights and glass or tile toppers keep sightlines open.
  • Segmented faces. Individual tiles can often be swapped, which makes refreshing a worn run of fabric simpler than replacing whole panels.
  • Integrated wire management. Raceways help keep cords off the floor, though powered components need a check by your electrician.
  • Familiar footprint. Standard 6'x6' and 6'x8' layouts translate easily to most floor plans.

What to check before you buy

Pre-owned panel systems reward a careful checklist. Walk through these with your quote in hand:

  1. Panel heights and widths. Confirm every size in the layout, including end-of-run and corner conditions.
  2. Connectors and hardware. Missing connectors can stall an install. Ask that hardware counts be listed on the inventory.
  3. Toppers. Glass, tile and fabric toppers look very different. Match what is already on your floor if you are expanding.
  4. Fabric and finish colors. Paint and fabric colorways vary between original orders. Request photos of the actual pieces.
  5. Worksurface depth and supports. Check that brackets and cantilevers are included for each surface.
  6. Power components. Base feeds and receptacles should be reviewed by a licensed electrician in your jurisdiction. Electrical work is not included in pricing.
  7. Storage. Overheads, pedestals and lateral files are often priced separately. Make sure keys or lock cores are accounted for.
Warranty reality check. Office Station sells pre-owned Knoll as an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, so rely on the condition grade, photos and a clear inventory rather than on the original factory warranty.

Expanding an existing Dividends layout

Many buyers are not starting from scratch. They have a floor of Dividends Horizon and need six or twelve more stations to match. That is a good use case for pre-owned, because buying new for a small expansion can mean long lead times and finishes that no longer line up with what you own.

To make matching easier, send the following:

  • Photos of a full station from two angles, plus a close-up of a panel connector
  • Panel heights measured from the floor
  • Worksurface sizes and edge profile
  • Fabric, tile and paint colors, or photos in natural light
  • The number of stations you need and a rough floor plan

If an exact match is not available, a mixed approach often works: matching frames and worksurfaces with toppers or tiles in a complementary finish.

Frequently asked questions

Is Office Station an authorized Knoll dealer?

No. Office Station is an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture, not an authorized Knoll dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, so the condition grade and your own inspection matter more than they would on a new order.

Can I add used Dividends Horizon to an existing Dividends layout?

Often, yes, as long as the panel heights, widths and connector hardware match what you already have. Send photos of your panels, connectors and power components along with a few measurements so the parts can be checked before you commit.

How much does a used 6'x6' Dividends Horizon workstation cost?

As a reference point for a 6'x6' workstation, furniture only: liquidation-grade runs about $615, remanufactured about $1,245 and new about $1,920. Final pricing depends on grade, finish, height and component list, and it excludes electrical, delivery and installation.
