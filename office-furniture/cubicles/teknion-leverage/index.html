Know the grade before you buy

Used Teknion Leverage Workstations, Graded A, B and C

Pre-owned Teknion Leverage comes in a range of conditions. Understanding what each grade looks like on panels, worksurfaces and storage helps you pay for condition only where it counts.

6 minute read

Teknion Leverage is a panel-based workstation system you will find in many corporate offices, and pre-owned Leverage gives you that commercial build at a fraction of new pricing. When you shop for used Teknion Leverage, the most important number on the quote after price is the condition grade. This page explains what those grades look like in practice, so you can read a quote with confidence and inspect a delivery against it.

How the grading scale works

Office Station uses three condition grades for pre-owned furniture. Grades describe appearance and wear. They are not a substitute for confirming that each station is complete and structurally sound, which every grade should be.

  • A, like new. Close to showroom appearance. You would need to look carefully to find wear.
  • B, very good. Light wear consistent with careful office use, generally not noticeable at normal viewing distance.
  • C, good. Visible wear that does not affect function. Priced for spaces where budget outranks appearance.

Wear shows up differently on each part of a workstation. Use this table as a practical reference when you compare stations or check a delivery.

Mixing grades on one floor

You rarely need grade A everywhere. A common approach is to map your floor into zones and assign a grade to each.

  1. Visible zones. Areas near entrances, conference rooms and client paths get grade A or remanufactured stations.
  2. Everyday zones. General team areas get grade B, which most employees will not distinguish from A.
  3. Utility zones. Training rooms, overflow seating and back-office work get grade C.

Keep each run a single grade where possible. Wear differences are easiest to spot when a grade A panel sits right beside a grade C one. Free space planning can help you draw those zones before you order. If your headcount is likely to grow, consider buying a few extra stations of the everyday grade now, since matching a specific grade and finish later depends on what inventory is available at that time.

Grade does not mean complete. A grade A station missing a connector or a worksurface support still cannot be installed. Ask for an itemized component list with your quote and check the delivery against both the list and the grade.

Delivery-day inspection checklist

Run through this list as stations arrive, before installation is underway, so any issue is caught while it is easy to fix.

  • Station count matches the quote, by grade.
  • Each station has its panels, connectors, supports and worksurfaces per the itemized list.
  • Panel heights match your plan.
  • Fabric and laminate condition matches the stated grade.
  • No damage from transit, especially on worksurface corners and edge banding.
  • Drawers open and close smoothly, and you know whether keys are included.
  • Power components are present as quoted, with electrical work scheduled with a licensed electrician.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between grade A, B and C?

Grade A is like new, grade B is very good and grade C is good. The difference is cosmetic: how much wear shows on fabric, laminate, edges and paint. Every grade should still be structurally sound and functional.

Is Office Station a Teknion dealer?

No. Office Station is an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture, not an authorized Teknion dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture.

How is remanufactured different from a grade?

A grade describes the condition of a pre-owned piece as it is. Remanufactured stations have been refreshed before sale and are priced separately, at $1,245 for a furniture-only 6x6 workstation versus $615 for liquidation-grade.

Can I mix grades in one order?

Yes, and it is often the best value. Many buyers put grade A or B stations in visible areas and grade C stations in back-office or training space. Ask for the grade to be noted per station on your itemized quote.

How soon can used Leverage stations be installed?

Liquidation-grade stations in stock can be ready in as little as 72 hours, and remanufactured stations usually take 7 to 14 days. Installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum.
