Used Herman Miller Canvas Workstations

Canvas is built for open, low-profile planning, which makes pre-owned sets a smart way to furnish a modern floor for less. Here is how to buy used Herman Miller Canvas with clear condition grades and no surprises.

6 minute read

Herman Miller Canvas is not a classic panel cubicle. It is a kit of walls, desks, storage and screens that lets you plan low, open neighborhoods with some privacy where it matters. That flexibility is exactly why used Canvas is worth a look: you can get a contemporary layout without paying for brand-new components.

This page explains how pre-owned Canvas is sourced and graded, what to check before you buy, and how pricing, delivery and installation work with Office Station.

Why teams buy used Herman Miller Canvas

Canvas tends to show up in offices that want a lighter, more residential feel than tall fabric panels. Desks attach to low walls or stand on their own, storage doubles as a divider, and screens add focus without closing people in. When a company downsizes or moves, those components often come back onto the market in good shape.

  • Open sightlines. Low walls keep natural light moving across the floor.
  • Mix of settings. The same family can cover individual desks, shared tables and storage walls.
  • Lower cost of entry. Pre-owned sets cost less than new, and in-stock inventory ships far faster.

What to check before buying used Canvas

Canvas is a system, so completeness matters as much as appearance. Use this checklist when you review a quote or walk a lot.

  1. Connecting hardware. Confirm the brackets, connectors and supports that join desks to walls are included. Missing hardware is the most common cause of install delays.
  2. Wall heights and lengths. Map the pieces to your plan. A lot of mostly short walls will not create the same privacy as taller ones.
  3. Worksurface edges. Look for chipped laminate or veneer on the front edge, where chairs and elbows hit.
  4. Power and data routing. Check whether cable management components are present. Note that electrical work is not part of furniture pricing.
  5. Finish consistency. Pieces from different installations can vary slightly in color. Ask whether a lot came from one site.
  6. Storage function. Open every drawer and door, and confirm locks and keys if you need them.

Frequently asked questions

Is Office Station an authorized Herman Miller dealer?

No. Office Station is an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture. We are not affiliated with or authorized by Herman Miller. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, so we describe each piece by our own condition grade.

What do the A, B and C condition grades mean for Canvas?

Grade A is like new, with little or no visible wear. Grade B is very good, with light signs of use such as minor scuffs on edges. Grade C is good and fully functional, with more visible cosmetic wear. We tell you the grade of each component group before you commit.

Can I add pieces to a Canvas layout I already own?

Often, yes, if matching components are available. Send us photos of your existing walls, desks and storage, plus finish and fabric details, and we will check what we can source. Exact color and finish matches are never guaranteed with pre-owned inventory.

How much does a used Canvas workstation cost?

Pricing depends on the configuration, component count and grade, so Canvas is quoted per project. As a reference point, our published 6'x6' workstation furniture-only prices are $615 liquidation-grade, $1,245 remanufactured and $1,920 new, excluding electrical, delivery and installation.

How fast can I get pre-owned Canvas furniture?

Liquidation-grade inventory that is in stock can be ready in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured furniture usually takes 7 to 14 days. If you need new furniture instead, plan on 4 to 6 weeks.
