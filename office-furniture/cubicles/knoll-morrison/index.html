Used Knoll Morrison Cubicles for Matching and Expansion

Morrison has been on office floors for a long time, which is exactly why pre-owned is often the most practical way to add to it. Here is how to match what you already own.

6 minute read

Most people searching for used Knoll Morrison cubicles are not designing a new office. They are trying to add stations to a floor that already has Morrison, replace damaged components, or rebuild a team area after a reorganization. This page is written for that situation.

The matching problem with legacy systems

Morrison is a long-running Knoll panel system, and floors that use it were often furnished in phases. That creates a few practical challenges when you need more:

  • Generational differences. Components from different eras may use different hardware or panel construction.
  • Finish drift. Fabric and paint from an original order may have faded or been discontinued.
  • Undocumented layouts. The original plans and part numbers are often long gone.
  • Small quantities. Ordering five stations new may not be practical, and finishes may not match anyway.

Pre-owned inventory solves some of these problems because it comes from floors built with similar components. The key is documenting what you have before you shop.

What to measure and photograph first

A little prep up front prevents a truck arriving with panels that will not connect. Gather the following before requesting a quote:

  1. Panel heights from the floor to the top of the panel, including any stacked sections.
  2. Panel widths for each size in a typical station.
  3. Connector close-ups showing how two panels join in a straight run, an L and a T.
  4. Base power photos if the panels are electrified. Your electrician should review any power components before use.
  5. Worksurface sizes and how they attach: brackets, cantilevers or panel-hung.
  6. Fabric and paint photographed in natural light next to a white sheet of paper.
  7. Storage pieces such as overheads, pedestals and lateral files, with dimensions.
  8. A rough floor plan of where the new stations will go, including columns and outlets.

An exact match is ideal when stations sit in the same run as existing ones. When new stations will be in a separate area, a different system can be more economical and faster to source.

Do not mix hardware blindly. Even within one brand, panels from different system lines or generations may not connect. Confirm compatibility with photos before you buy, not after the crew arrives.

Pre-owned stations carry a condition grade. Grade A is like new, B is very good and C is good. For stations that will sit beside older furniture, a B or C grade may actually blend in better than a like-new station.

Planning install day for an occupied floor

Expansions usually happen next to people who are still working. A few steps keep the disruption low:

  • Schedule around your team. Ask about after-hours or weekend installation if the new stations sit inside an active area.
  • Clear the path. Confirm dock access, freight elevator times and any building rules before delivery.
  • Stage by station. Label where each new station goes on the floor plan so parts are not left in a pile.
  • Book the electrician. If the new runs tie into existing powered panels, have your electrician scheduled for right after the furniture goes in.
  • Check the joins. Before the crew leaves, confirm the new panels sit plumb against existing runs with no gaps.

Frequently asked questions

Why buy used Knoll Morrison instead of switching systems?

If you already have a floor of Morrison, adding a few stations of the same system keeps the look consistent and avoids reconfiguring everything around a new product. Pre-owned is often the most direct route to parts and stations for an established legacy system.

Will used Morrison panels connect to my existing panels?

They can when panel heights, widths and connection hardware match. Morrison has been produced in more than one generation and configuration, so send photos of your panels, connectors and power components before ordering so compatibility can be checked.

What does a used 6'x6' Morrison workstation cost?

As a reference for a 6'x6' workstation, furniture only: liquidation-grade about $615, remanufactured about $1,245 and new about $1,920. Your price depends on grade, components and finish, and excludes electrical, delivery and installation.

Does the Knoll warranty come with used Morrison cubicles?

Generally no. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized Knoll dealer, and manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture.

Can I get just parts rather than full stations?

Ask. Add-ons like worksurfaces, panels or storage may be available depending on current inventory. Include exact sizes and photos in your request so the right components can be identified.
