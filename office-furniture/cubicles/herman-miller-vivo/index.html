Used Herman Miller Vivo Workstations

Vivo is a lighter-looking panel workstation that suits most everyday office floors. Buying it pre-owned works best when you match condition grade to each area and plan the layout before choosing a lot.

6 minute read

Herman Miller Vivo was designed as a lighter, more open take on the panel workstation. It covers the layouts most offices actually use, from rows of individual stations to small team pods, without the heavy look of older systems. That makes used Herman Miller Vivo a practical choice for companies that want a clean, current floor on a pre-owned budget.

This guide focuses on the two decisions that matter most with Vivo: which condition grade to buy for each part of your office, and how to plan the layout so the lot you choose actually fits.

Condition grades, and where each one belongs

Office Station sells pre-owned Herman Miller furniture as an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned pieces, so we grade every lot on its actual condition.

A mixed-grade plan is one of the easiest ways to save money with pre-owned Vivo. Put your best-looking stations where visitors walk, and use lower grades where appearance matters less. Just avoid mixing grades within a single visible run, where differences stand out.

Grade by zone, not by building. Tell us which areas are client-facing and which are not. We can price a floor with Grade A up front and Grade B or C behind the scenes, which often lowers the total without making the office look patched together.

How free space planning works

A lot of 40 Vivo stations only helps you if 40 stations fit your room with legal aisles and sensible circulation. Our space planning is free, and it follows a simple sequence:

  1. Send your space. A floor plan or a dimensioned sketch, plus headcount, columns, windows and power locations.
  2. Pick a station style. Common starting points are 6'x6' individual stations, smaller stations for task-based roles, or clusters for teams.
  3. Review the layout. We show how many stations fit, where aisles run and which panel heights go where.
  4. Match inventory. We align the plan with available pre-owned components and flag anything that needs another source.
  5. Confirm the quote. You get furniture, delivery and optional installation priced line by line.

Choosing panel heights

Panel height sets the tone of the floor. Lower panels keep light and conversation moving; taller ones give people more visual privacy. A common approach is lower panels facing windows and aisles, with taller panels on the sides between neighbors. Decide on this during planning, because a pre-owned lot may be weighted toward one height.

Liquidation-grade, remanufactured or new?

Condition grade and sourcing option are two different choices. Liquidation-grade furniture is pre-owned and sold as it comes out of an office, graded on its condition. Remanufactured furniture has been refreshed before sale and costs more, but it narrows the gap in appearance. New costs the most and takes the longest.

A practical rule: if your move-in date is close, start with in-stock liquidation-grade stations, which can be ready in as little as 72 hours. If you have a few weeks and want a more uniform look, remanufactured is usually the sweet spot. Choose new only when you need a specific finish or quantity that pre-owned supply cannot meet.

Quick inspection checklist for used Vivo

  • Every station includes its connectors, end treatments and worksurface supports.
  • Panel fabric is consistent in color across the run you plan to install together.
  • Worksurface edges are free of chips at the front edge.
  • Pedestals and overhead storage open smoothly, with keys if you need locks.
  • Power and cable management components are accounted for. Electrical work is priced separately from furniture.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of system is Herman Miller Vivo?

Vivo is a panel-based workstation system with a lighter, more open look than older, heavier panel lines. It supports familiar layouts such as rows of individual stations and team clusters. Because it plans much like other panel systems, it is straightforward to lay out on a standard office floor.

Should every workstation be the same condition grade?

Not necessarily. Many buyers put Grade A stations in visible or client-facing areas and Grade B or C stations in back-office zones to stretch the budget. The key is keeping each zone visually consistent, which we plan for.

Can Vivo connect to other Herman Miller panel systems?

Compatibility between different panel lines is not something to assume. Send us photos and details of what you already own, and we will tell you what we can confirm. When in doubt, plan separate zones rather than mixing systems within one run.

Is there a warranty on used Vivo?

Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized Herman Miller dealer. We grade every lot A like new, B very good or C good so expectations are clear.

How much does space planning cost?

Space planning is free. Send your floor plan or a dimensioned sketch, headcount and any fixed obstacles, and we will show how many stations fit and which configuration works. Installation, if you want it, is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum.
