Used Haworth Unigroup: Matching and Adding to Your Install

Unigroup is a legacy Haworth panel system still standing in many offices. If you need more seats, replacement parts or a few extra stations, the job is matching what you have.

6 minute read

Haworth Unigroup is one of those systems that outlasts the plans made for it. Offices installed it, grew, reorganized and kept building around it. If you are looking for used Haworth Unigroup today, you are probably not starting from scratch. You have stations on the floor and you need them to go further.

Office Station helps with that as an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture, not an authorized Haworth dealer. This page is about the practical work of matching parts, adding stations to an existing install and knowing when to stop adding.

Why legacy systems need a matching mindset

With a current product line, you can often specify exactly what you need. With a legacy system like Unigroup, you work with what exists. That changes the approach in a few ways.

  • Inventory drives options. Available panel sizes, fabrics and finishes depend on what pre-owned lots are on hand.
  • Generations can differ. A system in use for a long time may have components from different production periods and related Haworth lines. Parts that look alike may not connect the same way.
  • Wear varies. Your existing stations have aged in place. Incoming pre-owned components have aged somewhere else. Expect some difference.
Start with what is on your floor. Before shopping, pull the label information from a few existing panels, photograph a connector, a top cap and a work surface support, and note your panel heights. That record is what every match gets checked against.

Documenting your existing Unigroup install

A good match request answers these questions. Gather them once and you can reuse them for every future addition.

  1. Labels. What line and part information appears on the panel labels?
  2. Panel heights and widths. Measure each distinct size in your runs.
  3. Connectors. Photograph two-way, three-way and four-way joints, and any height transitions.
  4. Fabric and finish. Photograph panel fabric in natural light and note trim and frame colors.
  5. Work surfaces. Record surface sizes, shapes and how they attach.
  6. Storage. Note pedestal types, overheads and whether they hang from panels.
  7. Power. Note whether the panels are powered and where the feeds enter.
  8. Where the addition goes. Mark the area on a floor plan with dimensions.

Condition grades on pre-owned Unigroup

Components are graded A (like new), B (very good) or C (good). On a legacy system, Grade A stock can be harder to find simply because of age, so B is a realistic target for visible areas and C works well for interior or storage positions. Inspect fabric at chair height, frame straightness, connector condition and drawer function on a sample before a larger order. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture.

Add more, or replace?

Adding to Unigroup is usually the right call for a modest number of seats on a floor that still looks and works well. It becomes harder to justify as the addition grows. Price both paths when:

  • You need more stations than matching inventory can supply.
  • Existing fabric is stained, faded or worn through in visible areas.
  • Connectors, supports or storage are failing across the install.
  • Your team is moving toward a different station size or lower panels.

Practical tips for a smooth addition

  • Order a few spare connectors and top caps. Legacy parts are easier to buy once than to hunt for later.
  • Keep your documentation file updated after each addition so the next match is faster.
  • Stage and verify incoming parts before the install crew starts connecting to live runs.
  • Have a licensed electrician review any powered panels before connecting new sections.

Frequently asked questions

Can I still buy parts for Haworth Unigroup?

Unigroup is a legacy system, so pre-owned components are the practical source for most matching needs. Availability depends on incoming inventory. Send photos of your existing panels, labels and connectors so a match can be checked.

Will used Unigroup panels match my existing fabric?

An exact match is not guaranteed. Fabrics from different lots can vary in color and wear, and original fabrics may no longer be produced. Many offices place added stations as their own cluster or choose a coordinating fabric so small differences do not stand out.

Is Office Station an authorized Haworth dealer?

No. Office Station is an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture, not an authorized Haworth dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture.

When does it make sense to replace Unigroup instead of adding to it?

Replacement is worth pricing when existing stations are worn, when matching parts are scarce, or when your layout needs are changing significantly. Comparing both paths side by side, including any liquidation value of the old furniture, gives you a clear answer.

Can you work with an existing install outside Phoenix?

Pre-owned furniture can ship nationwide, with freight quoted per project. Greater Phoenix delivery is $195 central or $295 metro. Installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum.
