Used Haworth Premise Workstations, Reconfigured for Your Space

Buying pre-owned Premise is rarely about copying someone else's floor plan. It is about turning graded components into a layout that fits your team, your walls and your power.

6 minute read

Haworth Premise is a panel-based workstation system that shows up regularly on the pre-owned market. If you are shopping for used Haworth Premise, the good news is that panel systems are built to be disassembled and rebuilt. The catch is that the layout the furniture came out of is almost never the layout you need.

Office Station sells pre-owned Premise as an independent reseller, not an authorized Haworth dealer. Every component is graded, and free space planning helps you turn available inventory into stations that fit your floor.

How reconfiguration works

A reconfiguration project starts with your space, not the furniture. Here is the typical sequence.

  1. Measure and document. Send a floor plan or wall-to-wall dimensions, column locations, window lines and where power and data come into the space.
  2. Set the program. Decide headcount, station size, panel height preferences and how much storage each person needs.
  3. Draw the layout. A space plan arranges runs, clusters or rows, with clear aisles and room for growth.
  4. Build the parts list. The plan converts into panel widths and heights, connectors, work surfaces, supports, storage and trim.
  5. Match to inventory. The parts list is matched against graded pre-owned stock, and gaps are flagged before you commit.
  6. Install and connect. The crew builds the stations, and your licensed electrician verifies and connects any powered panels.
Your old layout is not your parts list. Changing from long rows to four-pack clusters, or from 8x8 to 6x6 stations, changes how many connectors and which panel widths you need. Always count from the new plan.

Condition grades on used Premise

Grades describe how each piece looks and works today. On a reconfiguration, you can place higher-grade components where they are most visible.

  • A, like new. Little or no visible wear. Good for reception-adjacent rows and client-facing teams.
  • B, very good. Light cosmetic wear, fully functional. The everyday choice for most open office areas.
  • C, good. Visible wear, structurally sound. Suited to interior rows, storage areas and budget-first projects.

Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Inspect a sample of panels, surfaces and storage before a large order, paying attention to fabric at chair height, laminate edges, drawer slides and locks.

Timing your project

Liquidation-grade stock can be ready in as little as 72 hours when in stock. Remanufactured furniture usually takes 7 to 14 days, and new furniture typically takes 4 to 6 weeks. On a reconfiguration, build in time for the space plan and parts match before those clocks start. If a move-in date is fixed, share it on the first call so the plan can work backward from it.

What to have ready for the first call

  • A floor plan, or dimensions with column and window locations marked.
  • Current and planned headcount, including expected growth over the next year.
  • Preferred station size and panel height, or the privacy level you want.
  • Where power and data enter the space, and whether your electrician is lined up.
  • Building rules for deliveries, such as dock hours, freight elevator reservations or after-hours work.
  • Photos of any Premise components you plan to reuse.

Reconfiguring Premise you already own

You may not need to buy a full layout. If your office already has Premise and your team or floor plan has changed, the space plan can reuse your existing components and fill only the gaps with pre-owned parts. Send photos of panel labels, connectors and trim so the match can be confirmed. If some of your furniture no longer fits the new plan, it may be worth something through office furniture liquidation.

Frequently asked questions

Is Office Station an authorized Haworth dealer?

No. Office Station is an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture, not an authorized Haworth dealer. Used Premise components are graded A, B or C so you know their condition.

Can used Premise workstations be reconfigured into a different layout?

Often, yes. Panel systems are designed to be taken down and rebuilt, but a new layout usually needs a different mix of panel widths, connectors and trim than the original. A component count against your new plan shows what is reusable and what must be added.

Do you charge for space planning?

No. Space planning is free. Share a floor plan or dimensions, your headcount and any power or data locations, and a layout can be drawn around the components available.

Does the manufacturer warranty apply to used Premise furniture?

Generally, no. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. The condition grade and inspection are your guide.

What does installation cost?

Professional installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum. Electrical work and delivery are separate. Greater Phoenix delivery is $195 central or $295 metro, and freight elsewhere is quoted per project.
