Two ways to buy

Remanufactured or pre-owned

We don't sell new seating. Every chair is either rebuilt to as-new condition or inspected and graded as it came off the floor.

Remanufactured

Stripped to the frame, re-upholstered, new cylinders, arm pads and casters as needed, then reassembled and tested. Presents as new and carries our written warranty.

Usually 7–14 days

Pre-owned

Pulled from a corporate floor, cleaned, mechanically inspected and graded A, B or C. The best value per seat and available in matched runs.

In stock: as little as 72 hours

Condition grades

How pre-owned chairs are graded

A

Like new

Minimal signs of use. Fabric, mesh and surfaces clean and intact; all mechanisms work smoothly.

B

Very good

Light, normal wear such as small scuffs or surface marks. Fully functional; cleaned and inspected.

C

Good

Visible wear or cosmetic blemishes. Fully functional and priced accordingly; a strong value for back-office use.

Questions

Seating FAQ

Are your pre-owned Aeron and Leap chairs authentic?

We source seating from corporate liquidations, not consumer marketplaces, and inspect manufacturer labels, date and serial tags and construction details before an item is listed. Replicas are common in the consumer market, so ask any seller how they verify authenticity.

Do pre-owned Herman Miller or Steelcase chairs have a warranty?

Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned seating. Office Station is an independent reseller, and any warranty we provide is stated in writing on your quote.

What do you inspect on a used ergonomic chair?

Gas cylinder lift and hold, tilt and recline tension, arm adjustments and pad wear, mesh or fabric condition, base and casters, and seat foam compression. Each chair is graded A, B or C.

Can I buy chairs in bulk for a whole office?

Yes. Corporate liquidations often yield dozens or hundreds of the same model, so matched sets for an entire floor are common. Volume quotes are available.

Do you buy used office chairs?

Yes. Designer seating is often the highest-value part of a liquidation. Use the liquidation estimator or request a walkthrough.
