Office Furniture Installation, Priced Per Workstation

Installation runs $95 per workstation with a $495 project minimum, and crews can work nights and weekends so your team never loses a workday. Here is exactly what that price covers and how to get your space ready.

6 minute read

Most installation quotes are hard to compare because they bundle labor, trucking, parts and a vague contingency into one number. Office Station keeps it simple: $95 per workstation, with a $495 minimum per project. You can do the math before you ever call us.

This page walks through what that rate includes, what it does not, how scheduling works, and how to prepare your space so install day goes smoothly.

A full budget example

Say you are outfitting 24 remanufactured 6'x6' workstations in central Greater Phoenix. Furniture at $1,245 per station is $29,880. What is included and what is not

Knowing the boundaries up front prevents change orders later.

Typical workstation installation tasks

Confirm the exact scope in your written quote. A workstation install generally covers:

  • Assembling panels, worksurfaces, supports and connectors to the approved layout
  • Mounting overhead storage, shelves and tackboards that are part of the station
  • Placing pedestals and freestanding storage at each station
  • Leveling and squaring runs so panels align and doors close cleanly
  • Removing packaging and staging debris from the work area

Not included

  • Electrical. Hardwiring powered panels to building power requires a licensed electrician.
  • Delivery. Priced separately by zone in Greater Phoenix or quoted as freight elsewhere.
  • Data cabling. Your IT or low-voltage vendor pulls and terminates cable.
  • Removal of existing furniture. If old furniture has to leave first, that is a decommissioning or liquidation scope, which can often offset your costs.
Sequence the electrician. The cleanest installs stand panels first, let the electrician make power connections, then set worksurfaces and storage. Book your electrician for the morning after install night so power is live before your team arrives.

Nights, weekends and building logistics

Installing during business hours means noise, blocked aisles and people working around a crew. Nights and weekends crews let you close the office Friday and open Monday with new stations ready.

Commercial buildings add their own rules. Office Station provides certificates of insurance and handles freight elevator and dock coordination with your property manager. What helps most from your side:

  1. Send us the building's contractor rules or move-in policy.
  2. Name one contact who can approve access and answer questions on install night.
  3. Confirm any additional insured names the building requires on the COI.
  4. Share whether the loading dock has height or length limits for trucks.

Prep checklist before install day

A crew can only move as fast as the space allows. Walk through this list about a week out.

  • Final layout approved and dimensions verified against the actual floor, not an old plan
  • Floor cleared of boxes, old furniture and personal items
  • Carpet or flooring work finished and cured
  • Floor or wall power locations marked and matching the layout
  • Painting and patching done so crews do not work around wet walls
  • IT told when stations will stand so cable can be pulled behind them
  • Building access, parking and elevator reservations confirmed in writing

If the floor plan is still uncertain, use the free space planning before you order. It is far cheaper to move a line on a drawing than to relocate a panel run.

Frequently asked questions

How much does office furniture installation cost?

Installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum per project. A 10-station install is $950, and a 4-station install is billed at the $495 minimum. Delivery and electrical work are priced separately.

Can you install after business hours?

Yes. Nights and weekends crews are available so installation does not interrupt your workday. Tell us your building's allowed work hours when you request a quote so we can schedule around them.

Does installation include electrical hookup?

No. Furniture prices and installation exclude electrical work. If your panels have powered raceways, a licensed electrician needs to make the connection to building power. Plan the electrician visit for after panels are standing.

What does the building need before crews arrive?

Most property managers ask for a certificate of insurance, a reserved freight elevator and a dock time. We handle COIs and freight elevator and dock coordination, but you should confirm the building's rules early, since some require requests several days in advance.

How do I request an installation quote?

Email hello@officestation.com with the number of workstations, the address, the allowed work hours and your target opening date. Include a floor plan if you have one.
