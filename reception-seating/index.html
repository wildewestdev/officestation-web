Used Reception Seating

Pre-owned reception and lounge seating from major commercial manufacturers, inspected and condition graded so you know what you are getting, at prices below new.

5 minute read

Reception seating takes more daily wear than almost anything else in an office, yet the best commercial lobby chairs are built to handle it for years. That is what makes used reception seating such a practical buy. You get the frames, proportions and materials of major brands, and you pay less than new.

Grading lets you spend where it shows. A lobby might use grade A lounge chairs up front and grade B guest chairs in a hallway waiting area around the corner.

Know the warranty reality. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, and Office Station is not an authorized dealer for these brands. What you get instead is a lower price and a written condition grade. Look closely at the pieces that matter most before you buy.

What to check on used reception chairs

  • Seat cushions. Press the front edge and center. Flattened foam shows first on the most-used seats.
  • Arm caps and arms. Hands rest here all day. Look for scuffs, cracks or worn upholstery.
  • Upholstery. Check seams, pilling and stains. Ask what the material is so you know how to clean it.
  • Frame and joints. Rock the chair gently. It should feel solid with no wobble or creak.
  • Glides and feet. Missing or worn glides can mark floors, and they are easy to overlook.
  • Quantity and match. Confirm fabric and finish are consistent across the chairs you are buying together.

Ask What Is In Stock

How many seats do you need?

Buying too many lobby chairs is one of the most common and expensive mistakes. Size your order by how many visitors are waiting at the same time, not by how many chairs fit along the wall.

  1. Find your busiest hour from calendars or visitor logs.
  2. Count the visits that overlap in that hour.
  3. Multiply by the average number of people who arrive per visit.
  4. Add one or two seats as a buffer for early arrivals.

Then plan the mix. A few upright chairs with arms help visitors who need support standing up, while a lounge grouping suits longer waits. Leave clear floor space for wheelchairs, strollers and bags, and keep the path from the door to the desk open. Accessibility and fire code clearances apply to your lobby, so confirm them with your building team.

Frequently asked questions

What brands of used reception seating does Office Station sell?

Office Station sells pre-owned furniture from brands including Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth, Humanscale, Teknion, Allsteel, Kimball, AIS and Friant. Specific reception and lounge models depend on current inventory, so ask what is available for your project.

Does used reception seating come with a warranty?

Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer, and manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. That is why every piece carries a condition grade and why inspecting upholstery and frames is worthwhile.

What do the condition grades mean?

Grade A is like new, grade B is very good and grade C is good. For client-facing lobbies, grade A is usually the best fit. Grades B and C often suit interview rooms, internal waiting areas and break-out spaces.

Can I get matching reception chairs?

Often, but it depends on what is in stock. Pre-owned seating usually comes in lots from a single office, so matching quantities are more likely for common guest chair styles. If you need an exact count, share it early and consider a coordinated mix as a backup.
