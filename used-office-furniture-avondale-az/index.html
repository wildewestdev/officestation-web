What to check before you order

  1. Measure the real space. Note column locations, door swings and where floor or wall power sits.
  2. Confirm access. Loading area, suite entry width, stairs or elevator, and any building hours for deliveries.
  3. Pick a grade by area. Decide where A or B condition matters and where C is perfectly fine.
  4. Ask about matching. If you are adding to existing furniture, confirm finish and component compatibility.
  5. Line up the electrician. Schedule power hookup for the same window as installation.
  6. Confirm your delivery zone on the written quote so the $195 or $295 rate is locked in.
