The Scottsdale market

Liquidating an office in Scottsdale

Scottsdale's office base skews toward headquarters, financial services and tech, much of it clustered around the Airpark and the Loop 101. Those tenants tend to buy premium systems furniture and designer seating, which is exactly the inventory that earns a payout instead of a removal bill.

Airpark buildings are often low-rise with ground-level or dock access, which keeps removal labor down. Old Town and Scottsdale Road suites are smaller footprints with higher finishes, so private-office casegoods and conference sets matter more than cubicle counts.

How quickly can you clear an office in Scottsdale?

Walkthroughs are typically scheduled within 48 hours and written offers follow within five business days. Removal is booked around your lease date; four weeks of notice gives the best pricing, and rush timelines under two weeks are possible with after-hours crews.
