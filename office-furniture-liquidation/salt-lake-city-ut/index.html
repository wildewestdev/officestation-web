The Salt Lake City market

Liquidating an office in Salt Lake City

Salt Lake City's office market runs from downtown towers to the Silicon Slopes tech corridor in Lehi and Draper, serving finance, software, healthcare and outdoor-industry headquarters. Tech-company restructuring and campus consolidations along the Wasatch Front release large installations of modern furniture.

Downtown towers bring freight-elevator and dock scheduling, while Silicon Slopes campuses are newer Class A buildings with large floor plates and docks. Winter snow shapes scheduling, and campus furniture from recent tech build-outs tends to be newer and higher value.

Questions

Salt Lake City liquidation FAQ

Do you pay for office furniture in Salt Lake City?

When it has resale value, yes. For example, 80 premium workstations under eight years old with typical seating, offices and conference rooms in Salt Lake City model out to roughly $5,250 to $7,600 paid to the company, before a walkthrough confirms it.

How quickly can you clear an office in Salt Lake City?

Walkthroughs are typically scheduled within two to three business days and written offers follow within five business days. Removal is booked around your lease date; four weeks of notice gives the best pricing, and rush timelines under two weeks are possible with after-hours crews.

Which Salt Lake City areas do you serve?

All of Salt Lake City, including Downtown Salt Lake City, Sugar House, Cottonwood Heights and Fort Union, Draper and Point of the Mountain, and ZIP codes such as 84101, 84111, 84106, 84115, 84121.

Can you handle building requirements in Salt Lake City office buildings?

Yes. We provide certificates of insurance and coordinate freight elevators, dock times, protection and after-hours access with building management before removal day.
