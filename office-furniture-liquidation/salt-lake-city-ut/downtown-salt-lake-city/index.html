About Downtown Salt Lake City

Clearing an office in Downtown Salt Lake City

Downtown Salt Lake City's Central Business District runs along Main Street and State Street between South Temple and 400 South, stretching west toward The Gateway and bordered by I-15. Law firms, banks, financial services firms, architects and engineers, government agencies and tech companies office in its towers.

The stock ranges from Class A towers like 222 Main and newer 650 Main to historic office buildings such as the 1907 Judge Building, plus office space at The Gateway. Towers typically require scheduled freight elevator and dock time plus detailed COIs, while historic buildings may have small elevators and limited loading, so a clear-out plan depends heavily on the specific building.

Planning a Downtown Salt Lake City move-out

What experienced facilities teams account for in this district before the first desk comes apart.

Full Salt Lake City liquidation guide

  1. Build winter weather buffers into schedules, since snowstorms can slow truck travel on I-15 and downtown streets.
  2. Plan loading around TRAX light rail on Main Street and South Temple, where tracks and platforms limit where trucks can stop.
  3. Reserve freight elevators and loading docks with tower management ahead of time, since many downtown buildings restrict moves to after hours.
  4. Check event calendars for the Delta Center and Salt Palace Convention Center, since games and conventions tighten downtown traffic and curb space.
  5. Take advantage of downtown's wide streets, but still confirm curb loading rules with building management before trucks arrive.
