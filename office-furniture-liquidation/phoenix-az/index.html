The Phoenix market

Liquidating an office in Phoenix

Phoenix holds the Valley's deepest stock of multi-tenant office towers, from the Central Avenue high-rises to the low-rise campuses along Deer Valley. That mix means move-outs range from a single law-firm suite to full corporate floors with freight-elevator reservations and security desks to coordinate.

Downtown and Midtown towers usually mean freight-elevator windows, loading-dock schedules and certificates of insurance naming building management. Camelback Corridor suites tend toward higher-end private offices and conference rooms, where premium seating and casegoods carry real resale value.

Process

How a Phoenix liquidation runs

  1. Within days

    Walkthrough

    We walk the space, count and grade the inventory, note access, building rules and your lease date. Typically within 48 hours in Greater Phoenix.

  2. Within 5 business days

    Written net offer

    One number: what the furniture is worth, minus what it costs to clear. Positive means we pay you.

  3. On your calendar

    Schedule

    Crews are booked around your move date, including nights and weekends when the building requires it.

  4. Removal day

    Clear and decommission

    Furniture, cabling, whiteboards and e-waste out, with building protection and dock scheduling handled.

  5. Handback

    Documentation

    Broom-clean space, photo walkthrough, certificates for e-waste and a diversion summary for your records.

Questions

Phoenix liquidation FAQ

Do you pay for office furniture in Phoenix?

When it has resale value, yes. For example, 80 premium workstations under eight years old with typical seating, offices and conference rooms in Phoenix model out to roughly $5,850 to $8,400 paid to the company, before a walkthrough confirms it.

How quickly can you clear an office in Phoenix?

Walkthroughs are typically scheduled within 48 hours and written offers follow within five business days. Removal is booked around your lease date; four weeks of notice gives the best pricing, and rush timelines under two weeks are possible with after-hours crews.

Which Phoenix areas do you serve?

All of Phoenix, including Downtown Phoenix, Midtown and the Central Avenue corridor, Camelback Corridor, Sky Harbor Center, and ZIP codes such as 85003, 85004, 85012, 85016, 85018.

Can you handle building requirements in Phoenix office buildings?

Yes. We provide certificates of insurance and coordinate freight elevators, dock times, protection and after-hours access with building management before removal day.
