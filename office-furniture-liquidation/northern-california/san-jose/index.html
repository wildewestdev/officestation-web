About San Jose

Clearing an office in San Jose

San Jose's office market splits between downtown towers around Almaden Boulevard, Santa Clara Street and SAP Center, and the North San Jose tech corridor along North First Street and Tasman Drive near San Jose Mineta International Airport. Semiconductor, hardware, software, medical device and professional services firms office here along SR-87, US-101, I-880 and the VTA light rail line.

Downtown has mid-rise and high-rise towers with managed docks, freight elevators and garage loading, while North San Jose is mostly low-rise R&D and office campuses with dock-high doors and big surface lots. Many North San Jose buildings combine offices with labs, test areas or light manufacturing, so furniture must be separated from equipment before the truck is loaded.

Full Northern California liquidation guide

  1. Downtown towers typically require a certificate of insurance and a reserved freight elevator window, often after hours.
  2. Check the SAP Center schedule for downtown jobs and the Levi's Stadium calendar for North San Jose jobs near Tasman Drive, since event traffic backs up nearby streets and ramps.
  3. Keep trucks off the VTA light rail tracks on North First Street and in the downtown transit mall.
  4. In R&D campuses, confirm which benches, racks and test equipment are company assets for separate handling before furniture crews start.
  5. Servers, monitors and test gear need a certified e-waste path, because California DTSC rules bar covered electronic devices from landfill and drives need destruction certificates.
