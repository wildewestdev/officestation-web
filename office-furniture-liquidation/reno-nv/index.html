The Reno market

Liquidating an office in Reno

Reno's office market is smaller and tighter than most in the West, serving logistics, advanced manufacturing, tech, healthcare and professional services. Low vacancy means tenants who outgrow a suite often relocate across town, and the furniture rarely moves with them.

Most Reno and Sparks office space is low-rise and suburban, especially in Meadowood and South Meadows, with straightforward ground or dock access. Winter snow and ice make scheduling and building protection part of the plan from November through March.

Questions

Reno liquidation FAQ

Do you pay for office furniture in Reno?

When it has resale value, yes. For example, 80 premium workstations under eight years old with typical seating, offices and conference rooms in Reno model out to roughly $5,250 to $7,600 paid to the company, before a walkthrough confirms it.

How quickly can you clear an office in Reno?

Walkthroughs are typically scheduled within two to three business days and written offers follow within five business days. Removal is booked around your lease date; four weeks of notice gives the best pricing, and rush timelines under two weeks are possible with after-hours crews.

Which Reno areas do you serve?

All of Reno, including Downtown Reno and the Riverwalk, Meadowood, South Meadows, Midtown Reno, and ZIP codes such as 89501, 89502, 89509, 89511, 89521.

Can you handle building requirements in Reno office buildings?

Yes. We provide certificates of insurance and coordinate freight elevators, dock times, protection and after-hours access with building management before removal day.
