About South Meadows

Clearing an office in South Meadows

South Meadows is south Reno's suburban office and business park district along I-580, centered on South Meadows Parkway, Double R Boulevard and Double Diamond Parkway near Damonte Ranch. It houses healthcare and medical office users, gaming technology, financial services and regional corporate offices.

Buildings are mostly one- to three-story suburban office and flex properties in business parks such as Double Diamond, many with surface parking and some with grade-level or dock doors in flex space. That usually makes truck access easier than downtown, but furniture removal still needs coordination with park management for staging, dumpster placement and any after-hours rules.

Planning a South Meadows move-out

What experienced facilities teams account for in this district before the first desk comes apart.

Full Reno liquidation guide

  1. Watch winter forecasts closely, since snow and ice on I-580 and south Reno arterials can push back a removal day.
  2. Stage trucks in business park lots with management approval so loading does not block shared parking for medical and office neighbors.
  3. Use grade-level or dock doors in flex buildings when available, which can speed removal compared with carrying through a front lobby.
  4. Schedule around I-580 commute peaks at the South Meadows Parkway and Damonte Ranch Parkway interchanges.
  5. Confirm COI requirements for medical office buildings early, since healthcare landlords often have stricter vendor and after-hours access rules.
