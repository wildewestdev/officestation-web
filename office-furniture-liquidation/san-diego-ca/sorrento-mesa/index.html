About Sorrento Mesa

Clearing an office in Sorrento Mesa

Sorrento Mesa is the tech and life-science mesa between I-805, Mira Mesa Boulevard and Carroll Canyon Road, home to Qualcomm's headquarters campus and dozens of biotech, wireless and defense firms. Offices and labs line Barnes Canyon Road, Pacific Center Boulevard and Sorrento Valley Road down toward the Sorrento Valley Coaster station.

The stock is mostly one- to three-story suburban office and R&D buildings with surface parking and grade-level or dock-high loading, mixed with converted lab space. Clearing a floor here usually means separating office furniture from lab benches, fume hoods and equipment that may belong to the landlord or require a decommissioning contractor.

Planning a Sorrento Mesa move-out

What experienced facilities teams account for in this district before the first desk comes apart.

Full San Diego liquidation guide

  1. Low-rise buildings with surface lots let crews stage trucks close to the door, but confirm with the landlord whether shared lots must stay open for neighboring tenants during the day.
  2. In lab conversions, get the tenant's lab decommissioning or clearance sign-off before furniture crews enter so chemical and biological areas are already released.
  3. Avoid the Mira Mesa Boulevard and I-805 interchange at peak commute times, which is the main chokepoint in and out of the mesa.
  4. Separate servers, monitors and test equipment from furniture early, since California DTSC rules keep covered electronic devices out of landfill and data-bearing devices need destruction certificates.
  5. Ask for a diversion summary if the company reports sustainability numbers, as many life-science tenants here track waste diversion.
