About Boulder

Clearing an office in Boulder

Boulder's office market spans downtown around the Pearl Street Mall, the East Boulder and Flatiron Park area, and the corridors along 28th Street, Walnut Street and the Diagonal Highway, about 25 miles northwest of Denver via US-36. Outdoor and consumer brands, software and tech firms, research and aerospace companies, and University of Colorado spinoffs are the core tenants.

Downtown Boulder has low-rise office buildings with strict height limits, many with retail below and limited loading, while East Boulder offers one- and two-story office, R&D and flex buildings with grade-level access. Downtown clear-outs usually hinge on alley and curb access, while flex buildings may have dock or roll-up doors but can include lab or specialized equipment that needs separate handling.

Full Denver liquidation guide

  1. Route all computers, monitors and printers to certified electronics recyclers, because Colorado has banned disposal of covered electronic devices in landfills since July 1, 2013.
  2. Plan around US-36 commute congestion between Denver and Boulder, and allow extra time during winter storms.
  3. Coordinate alley and curb loading around the Pearl Street Mall, where pedestrian zones and retail deliveries limit where trucks can stop.
  4. Separate lab benches, R&D equipment and furniture early in East Boulder flex spaces, since specialized equipment often needs its own removal path.
  5. Account for University of Colorado move-in and game-day traffic when scheduling removals in late August and fall.
