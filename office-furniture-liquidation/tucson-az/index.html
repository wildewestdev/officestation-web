The Tucson market

Liquidating an office in Tucson

Tucson's office market is anchored by healthcare, the University of Arizona, government and defense-related employers, with offices along the Broadway corridor, Downtown and the Foothills. Healthcare expansions and consolidations drive much of the local move activity.

Most Tucson office space is low-rise and mid-rise, with medical office buildings and suburban complexes that offer easy ground access. Downtown mid-rises and university-adjacent buildings add loading-zone and scheduling rules, and summer heat favors early-morning work.

Questions

Tucson liquidation FAQ

Do you pay for office furniture in Tucson?

When it has resale value, yes. For example, 80 premium workstations under eight years old with typical seating, offices and conference rooms in Tucson model out to roughly $5,250 to $7,600 paid to the company, before a walkthrough confirms it.

How quickly can you clear an office in Tucson?

Walkthroughs are typically scheduled within two to three business days and written offers follow within five business days. Removal is booked around your lease date; four weeks of notice gives the best pricing, and rush timelines under two weeks are possible with after-hours crews.

Which Tucson areas do you serve?

All of Tucson, including Downtown Tucson, Midtown and the Broadway corridor, Catalina Foothills, Northwest Tucson and Oro Valley, and ZIP codes such as 85701, 85705, 85711, 85712, 85716.

Can you handle building requirements in Tucson office buildings?

Yes. We provide certificates of insurance and coordinate freight elevators, dock times, protection and after-hours access with building management before removal day.
