Used Office Furniture in Queen Creek, AZ

Queen Creek sits at the far southeast edge of Greater Phoenix, so delivery is a real line on your budget. Here is how to figure the landed cost of pre-owned office furniture before you request a quote.

6 minute read

Queen Creek has grown fast, and a lot of the offices opening there are small and practical: medical and dental suites, insurance and real estate offices, contractors, and professional firms moving into newer commercial space. If you are searching for used office furniture in Queen Creek, AZ, you likely want brand-name quality at a pre-owned price, and you want to know what it actually costs once it is standing in your suite.

That second part is where most furniture budgets drift. The sticker price per workstation is only one of four lines. This page lays out all four so you can build a realistic number for a Queen Creek address.

The four lines of a landed cost

Office Station prices each piece of the job separately. That keeps the quote easy to read and makes it obvious where you can save.

  1. Furniture. A 6'x6' workstation, furniture only, is $615 liquidation-grade, $1,245 remanufactured or $1,920 new.
  2. Delivery. Greater Phoenix delivery is $195 in the central zone or $295 in the metro zone, charged per delivery rather than per station.
  3. Installation. Professional installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum.
  4. Electrical. Not included. If your stations use powered panels or need hardwiring, plan on your own electrician or your landlord's.

Because Queen Creek is on the outer edge of the Valley, do not assume the central rate. Budget with the $295 metro figure until your written quote confirms the zone for your address. If it comes back central, you saved $100.

Small orders and the install minimum

If you only need four stations for a new suite, installation at $95 each would be $380, but the $495 minimum applies. Four liquidation-grade stations would come to $2,460 in furniture, $495 installed and $295 delivered, for about $3,250 before electrical. The per-station install cost is higher at that size, so if a fifth or sixth station is already on your hiring plan, it can make sense to include it now rather than paying for a second trip later.

Choosing a tier for your Queen Creek space

Each tier fits a different kind of office. Think about who sits at the desks and who walks past them.

  • Liquidation-grade is furniture that came out of a corporate space and is sold with a condition grade: A like new, B very good or C good. It suits back-office teams, startups and any office that needs to open quickly.
  • Remanufactured stations are brought to a consistent standard as a matched set. They suit client-facing offices where a uniform look matters, such as a front office that patients or customers see.
  • New is the right call when you need a specific finish or configuration that pre-owned inventory does not have, and you can wait 4 to 6 weeks.

Pre-owned brands in inventory can include Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth, Humanscale, Teknion, Allsteel, Kimball, AIS and Friant. Office Station sells these as an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer, and manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Before delivery day in Queen Creek

Newer commercial buildings are often easier to deliver into than older ones, but a few checks still prevent a wasted trip:

  • Confirm suite access hours and whether your landlord needs a certificate of insurance on file.
  • Measure door widths and any hallway turns between the parking lot and your suite.
  • Make sure flooring, paint and data cabling are finished before furniture arrives.
  • Line up your electrician if the layout needs power at the stations.
  • Have a final head count and layout signed off so the install crew builds it once.

Frequently asked questions

Does Office Station deliver used office furniture to Queen Creek?

Yes. Queen Creek is part of Greater Phoenix, where delivery is priced by zone: $195 for the central zone and $295 for the metro zone. Your exact address sets the zone, and it is confirmed in writing on your quote.

What is the total cost of a used workstation delivered to Queen Creek?

Start with the furniture: a 6'x6' workstation is $615 liquidation-grade, $1,245 remanufactured or $1,920 new. Add installation at $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum, plus one delivery charge for the order. Electrical work is not included and is handled by your own electrician or building.

Why does the installation minimum matter for small Queen Creek offices?

Installation is $95 per workstation, but the minimum is $495. That means any order of five stations or fewer pays the $495 minimum, so the per-station install cost is higher on very small orders. Planning a slightly larger order in one delivery can spread that cost.

How quickly can furniture arrive in Queen Creek?

Liquidation-grade stock can be ready in as little as 72 hours when it is in stock. Remanufactured workstations usually take 7 to 14 days, and new workstations take 4 to 6 weeks.
