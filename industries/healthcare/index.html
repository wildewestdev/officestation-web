Medical Office Furniture for the Business Side of Your Clinic

Billing, scheduling, records and front-desk teams work long days at their stations. Pre-owned seating, workstations and filing furnish those spaces well for less, and after-hours decommissioning clears old space without canceling a single appointment.

6 minute read

Medical office furniture usually brings exam tables and procedure chairs to mind. But every practice, clinic and health system also runs a full business operation: front desk, scheduling, billing, coding, records, referrals and management. Those teams sit for long shifts, handle a lot of paper and screens, and need furniture that holds up.

That administrative side is where Office Station helps. You get pre-owned and remanufactured commercial office furniture for admin areas, and decommissioning for the space you are leaving. Office Station does not supply clinical or exam room furniture.

What admin spaces in healthcare actually need

Clinic admin areas are different from a typical corporate office in a few ways. Space is tight, since square footage goes to patient care first. Staff often work rotating shifts at shared stations. And privacy matters, both for conversations and for what is on screen.

Seating for long shifts

If there is one place not to cut corners, it is task seating for staff who sit most of the day. The good news is that designer task chairs are among the best values in pre-owned furniture. Office Station carries pre-owned seating from brands including Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth and Humanscale.

When comparing chairs, check for:

  • Adjustable seat height and depth, so one chair can fit staff on different shifts
  • Adjustable arms that clear the worksurface edge
  • Lumbar support that can be tuned rather than fixed
  • Upholstery that can be wiped down, if your infection control team has a preference
  • Condition grade: A like new, B very good, C good

Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer, so manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned chairs. Browse options on the pre-owned office chairs page.

Decommissioning a clinic without canceling patients

Practices consolidate, health systems move admin teams off-site, and leases end. The hard part is not the furniture. It is doing the work while patients are still being seen down the hall, or in a medical office building shared with other practices.

In Office Station's liquidation markets, office decommissioning is built for that:

  • Nights and weekends crews so removal happens after the last patient leaves
  • COIs issued to your building and property manager
  • Freight elevator and dock coordination so crews stay out of patient elevators and entrances
  • Itemized inventory of everything removed
  • E-waste handling with certificates of data destruction for workstations and devices
  • Photo-documented, broom-clean handback for your landlord
  • Diversion summary showing where materials went
Loop in compliance before the crew arrives. Certificates of data destruction document how devices were handled, but your compliance officer should confirm what your organization requires for anything that stored patient information. Also walk every file room and drawer yourself. Paper records left in a cabinet are your responsibility, not the furniture's.

What you get back

You receive a written net offer: the resale value of the furniture minus the cost to remove it. A positive number means you are paid. Walkthroughs typically happen within 48 hours in Greater Phoenix and 2-3 business days in other markets, with offers within 5 business days. Pre-move checklist for clinic admin areas

  1. Confirm with IT which devices are leaving and which are moving.
  2. Empty and inspect every file cabinet, pedestal and drawer for paper records.
  3. Get the building's COI wording and after-hours rules from the property manager.
  4. Reserve the freight elevator and dock for the removal window.
  5. Decide which furniture moves with you, which is liquidated and what you need to buy.
  6. Book electrical for any powered workstations in the new space.

If you are relocating rather than closing, corporate relocation ties the move, the liquidation and the new furniture together. Questions go to hello@officestation.com or the contact page.

Frequently asked questions

Does Office Station sell exam room or clinical furniture?

No. Office Station focuses on office furniture for the administrative side of healthcare: workstations, task and guest seating, conference tables and filing. Exam tables, clinical seating and casework should come from a medical equipment supplier.

Can old furniture be removed without closing the clinic?

Yes, in Office Station's liquidation markets. Nights and weekends crews can work after patients leave, with COIs, freight elevator and dock coordination handled for medical office buildings. You get a photo-documented broom-clean handback.

What happens to old computers that may hold patient data?

E-waste is handled with certificates of data destruction, which you should keep with your compliance records. Talk with your compliance officer before the project about any additional steps your organization requires for devices that stored patient information.

What does a pre-owned workstation cost for a billing or scheduling team?

Do warranties transfer on pre-owned seating?

Generally no. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer, and manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Condition grades A like new, B very good and C good tell you what shape each piece is in.
