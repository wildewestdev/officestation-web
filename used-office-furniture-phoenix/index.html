Used Office Furniture in Phoenix, AZ

Phoenix is where buying and selling used office furniture moves fastest. Get zone delivery at $195 or $295, in-stock workstations in as little as 72 hours and liquidation walkthroughs typically within 48 hours.

6 minute read

Phoenix is the center of Office Station's service area, and that changes how buying used office furniture works here. Delivery is priced by zone instead of quoted as freight. Liquidation walkthroughs typically happen within 48 hours, compared with 2 to 3 business days in other markets. And when offices move, expand or consolidate, you can use the same team to buy furniture, sell it, or both.

Use this page as your starting point for any Phoenix-area project, whether you are furnishing a new suite, clearing an old one or doing both at once.

What you can buy in Phoenix

Office Station sells pre-owned furniture from Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth, Humanscale, Teknion, Allsteel, Kimball, AIS and Friant. It is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer, and manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned pieces.

Why zone delivery matters. Outside Greater Phoenix, freight is quoted per project. Inside it, you know the delivery number before you ask: $195 central or $295 metro. That makes small orders, like a handful of chairs or a few stations, far more practical to buy used.

Cubicle pages across the metro

Phoenix is the hub, but the same zone delivery, installation pricing and lead times apply across Greater Phoenix. Dedicated cubicle pages cover Scottsdale, Tempe, Chandler, Mesa, Gilbert, Glendale and Peoria, and you can reach each of them from the main used cubicles page.

The city you are in affects two practical things. First, whether your address falls in the $195 central zone or the $295 metro zone, which your quote confirms. Second, the kind of building you are moving into. A downtown or midtown tower often means reserved elevator time, a loading dock schedule and stricter vendor insurance rules. A single-story suite in a business park usually means simpler access but may still need parking or gate coordination. Tell the team which you have so the delivery plan matches the building.

Choosing a tier for a Phoenix office

Because delivery is predictable here, the main decision is which workstation tier to buy. A simple way to think about it:

  • Liquidation-grade fits when the start date is close, the budget is tight or the room is a training, overflow or back-office space. Selection depends on what is in stock.
  • Remanufactured fits most teams that want a refreshed, consistent look and can wait one to two weeks.
  • New fits when you need a specific finish or configuration and have four to six weeks of runway.

Mixing tiers in one order is common. Put the higher tier where clients and candidates walk through, and save on the rooms they never see.

Selling furniture: the 48-hour walkthrough

The same flow of offices that supplies pre-owned inventory also creates demand for fast, organized removal. In Greater Phoenix, Office Station liquidation works like this:

  1. Request. Share your address, rough furniture counts and move-out date, or start with the liquidation estimator.
  2. Walkthrough. Typically within 48 hours. The team reviews brands, condition, quantities and building access.
  3. Written net offer. Within 5 business days. The offer equals resale value minus removal cost. Positive means you are paid.
  4. Removal. Scheduled around your building, with night and weekend crews, certificates of insurance, and freight elevator and dock coordination.
  5. Handback. A photo-documented broom-clean space, an itemized inventory, certificates of data destruction for e-waste and a diversion summary.

Resale value is driven by brand, condition and how many matching pieces you have. Removal cost is driven by labor, access and disposal. Well-kept systems furniture in matching quantities tends to produce the strongest offers, while mixed or heavily worn furniture may come out closer to a cleanout cost. Either way, you see the number in writing before anything is scheduled.

Phoenix buyer checklist

  • Confirm your delivery zone and whether your building needs a dock or elevator reservation.
  • Ask property management which certificate of insurance details they require for vendors.
  • Plan deliveries and installs around building hours, especially in high-rise towers with restricted move times.
  • Decide which rooms need Grade A and where Grade B or C is fine.
  • Line up an electrician for powered panels before installation day.
  • Check whether your count is close to a 20, 50 or 100-station volume threshold.
