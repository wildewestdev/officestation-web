Questions

Frequently asked

Do you really pay companies for their office furniture?

When the inventory has resale value, yes. Premium systems furniture and designer seating in good condition is often worth more than the labor to remove it, so the net goes to you. Older or unbranded furniture usually lands at no cost or a disclosed removal fee, and you see that number in writing before anything moves.

How large a project can Office Station take on?

We plan projects from single suites to full multi-floor headquarters. Crew count scales with square footage and deadline, and large projects run in phases around your move schedule, including nights and weekends.

What areas do you serve?

Office liquidation and decommissioning in Phoenix, Tucson, Flagstaff, Las Vegas, Reno, San Diego, Orange County, Northern California, Denver, Salt Lake City and Albuquerque. Pre-owned furniture and cubicle delivery covers Greater Phoenix. Projects elsewhere are quoted with travel included.

Can you handle the decommissioning, not just the furniture?

Yes. Decommissioning covers furniture, low-voltage cabling, whiteboards, signage, appliances and e-waste with certificates of data destruction, coordinated to your landlord's restoration requirements.

Is the pre-owned furniture you sell really designer brands?

Much of our inventory comes straight from corporate floors furnished with Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth and similar manufacturers. Every piece is inspected and graded, and listings state brand, model and condition. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer for those manufacturers.

How fast can you install furniture for a new office?

In-stock liquidation-grade furniture can be delivered and installed in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured furniture usually takes 7 to 14 days, and new commercial lines typically take 4 to 6 weeks.
