Relocation FAQ

Is it cheaper to move our furniture or buy pre-owned at the new office?

It depends on the furniture and the new floor plan. Systems furniture often doesn't fit a new layout without new parts, so moving it can cost more than liquidating it and installing matched pre-owned furniture. We price both options side by side.

Can you move, store and reinstall furniture?

Yes. We disassemble, move, store short-term if dates don't line up, and reinstall and reconfigure at the new space.

Can you work after hours to avoid downtime?

Yes. Most relocations run evenings and weekends so teams leave on Friday and sit down at working desks on Monday.

What happens to the furniture we don't take?

It is liquidated in the same project, and its resale value is credited against the relocation cost.
