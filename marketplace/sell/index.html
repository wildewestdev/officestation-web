OfficeStation™ Marketplace · Consignment

Sell your office furniture without the hassle

Send us photos and a few details. Office Station prices it, lists it on the Marketplace, handles every buyer, delivers and installs it, and pays you when it sells.

  1. Step 1

    Send photos and details

    A few phone photos of the furniture and roughly how many pieces. Takes five minutes.

  2. Step 2

    We price and confirm terms

    We set the price from current market data and confirm consignment terms with you in writing.

  3. Step 3

    We list and sell

    Your listing goes live on the OfficeStation Marketplace; we answer buyers and schedule viewings.

  4. Step 4

    We move it, you get paid

    Our crews pick up, deliver and install. You're paid when it sells.

What does it cost to list?

Nothing up front. Office Station earns a share of the sale; the exact terms are confirmed with you in writing before your listing goes live.

Who sets the price?

Office Station does, using current resale data for your brand, model, condition and quantity. Pricing it right is how it sells quickly.

What sells best?

Complete, matched sets of cubicles and workstations, name-brand task chairs (Herman Miller, Steelcase, Haworth, Knoll, Humanscale), and clean desks and storage. Mixed or partial sets can still sell; we'll tell you honestly.

What if I need the whole office cleared?

That's our liquidation service: one written net number for everything, removal included. See office furniture liquidation.
