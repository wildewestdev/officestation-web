Used Office Furniture in Chandler, AZ

A Chandler office is more than a row of cubicles. Here is how to furnish work areas, private offices, meeting rooms and seating with pre-owned designer furniture, and what it costs to get it delivered and installed.

6 minute read

When people search for used office furniture in Chandler, they are usually furnishing an entire office, not a single room. Workstations get most of the attention, but chairs, private office desks, conference tables and storage add up quickly. This page walks through each part of a Chandler office and how to buy it pre-owned without surprises.

If workstations are your main need, start with the dedicated used cubicles in Chandler page. Everything below covers the whole floor.

Room-by-room guide

Open work areas

Workstations are priced in three tiers. A 6'x6' station, furniture only, is $615 liquidation-grade, $1,245 remanufactured or $1,920 new. Prices exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation. Lead times run from as little as 72 hours for in-stock liquidation-grade, to usually 7 to 14 days for remanufactured, to 4 to 6 weeks for new.

Seating

Seating is the one purchase every employee touches all day. Pre-owned task chairs from Herman Miller, Steelcase, Humanscale and Haworth give people adjustable, well-built seating at a fraction of new cost. Before you buy, confirm that the adjustments you care about are present and working: seat height, tilt tension, arm height and lumbar. Browse pre-owned office chairs.

Private offices

Desks, returns, credenzas and bookcases for managers and executives are available through the pre-owned office furniture catalog. Private offices are visible to visitors, so this is usually where grade A or B is worth the difference.

Meeting and training rooms

Conference tables and guest seating are durable categories that often hold up well in the used market. Measure the room including chair clearance, and check where floor boxes or wall power sit if the table needs power or data.

Storage and filing

Lateral files and storage cabinets are straightforward to buy used. Check that drawers run smoothly and that keys or lock cores are available.

Delivery and installation in Chandler

Greater Phoenix delivery is a published flat rate: $195 for central zones and $295 for the wider metro. Your quote confirms the zone for your Chandler address. Installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum, and space planning is free.

Before delivery day, confirm the practical details with your building:

  • Loading dock or delivery entrance, and any reserved hours
  • Elevator access and whether padding or a reservation is required
  • Suite door widths for conference tables and large desks
  • Whether the landlord requires a certificate of insurance from vendors
  • When your electrician will connect powered workstations, since electrical is not included

Building a realistic Chandler timeline

Lead times differ by tier, so a mixed order moves at the pace of its slowest item. If you combine in-stock liquidation-grade chairs with remanufactured workstations, plan around the 7 to 14 day remanufactured window rather than the 72-hour one. Anything ordered new sets a 4 to 6 week clock.

A practical sequence for most Chandler offices looks like this:

  1. Request free space planning and confirm station count.
  2. Choose tiers and condition grades by room.
  3. Book your electrician for the week of installation.
  4. Confirm building access, insurance requirements and delivery hours.
  5. Schedule delivery and installation together so stations go up the day they arrive.

If you have a hard move-in date, share it up front. It is easier to shift a few items to a faster tier early than to rush them later.
