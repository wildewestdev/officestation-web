Used Healthcare Office Furniture

Clinics and practices can furnish the non-clinical side of the building with graded pre-owned office furniture, then close or consolidate old sites with documented e-waste handling.

6 minute read

In a healthcare organization, the money should go to patient care. That is exactly why used healthcare office furniture makes sense for the parts of a clinic patients rarely think about: the billing office, the scheduling team, provider offices, the break room and the front desk. Graded pre-owned furniture from major manufacturers costs far less than new, arrives faster and can be planned around clinic hours.

This page covers where pre-owned furniture fits in a healthcare setting, how to compare options and what to expect when you close, move or consolidate a location.

Where pre-owned office furniture belongs in a clinic

Think of the building in two zones. Clinical rooms often need purpose-built medical furniture chosen with your infection prevention team. Administrative and staff zones use standard commercial office furniture, which is where pre-owned delivers the most value.

Pricing and lead times

Using a standard 6'x6' workstation as the benchmark for a billing or scheduling team:

  • New: $1,920, typically 4 to 6 weeks.

Prices are furniture only and exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation. Installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum. Volume pricing takes 5% off at 20 or more stations, 8% at 50 or more and 12% at 100 or more, which helps health systems furnishing several sites at once.

Opening a new practice or satellite clinic

New locations are where timelines get tight. The lease is signed, the buildout is scheduled and the opening date is already on the marketing calendar, but new furniture can take 4 to 6 weeks. A practical approach is to plan the admin layout early with our free space planning, order remanufactured pieces for reception and provider offices once walls are set, and fill back-office areas with liquidation-grade stock closer to opening. That keeps furniture from arriving before the flooring is finished and still gets staff seated on day one.

If you are expanding to several sites, keep one standard workstation and chair model so staff who float between clinics find the same setup everywhere.

Closing or consolidating a clinic location

When a practice merges, a lease ends or a health system consolidates offices, the old space still has to be emptied and handed back. Office Station's office decommissioning service covers the non-clinical contents of the space.

  1. Walkthrough. Typically within 48 hours in Greater Phoenix and 2 to 3 business days in our other markets.
  2. Written net offer. Within 5 business days. The offer equals the resale value of the furniture minus removal cost. If it is positive, you are paid.
  3. Scheduling around patients. Night and weekend crews, with freight elevator and dock coordination in medical office buildings.
  4. Insurance. Certificates of insurance issued to building management before work begins.
  5. E-waste. Computers and electronics handled as e-waste with certificates of data destruction.
  6. Handback. An itemized inventory, a photo-documented broom-clean handback and a diversion summary.

Medical equipment, pharmaceuticals, biohazard waste and paper records follow their own regulated disposal paths, so plan for those separately with the appropriate specialists before furniture removal day.

Pre-project checklist for practice managers

  • Map which rooms are clinical and which are administrative.
  • Have compliance confirm requirements for any furniture in patient areas.
  • Confirm that paper records and regulated items are removed or scheduled separately.
  • Inventory computers, printers and other devices that may hold data.
  • Get COI requirements and move-in or move-out rules from building management.
  • Pick after-hours windows that do not conflict with early or late appointments.
  • Send floor plans for the new space so free space planning can start early.

Frequently asked questions

Is pre-owned office furniture suitable for patient care areas?

Office Station sells commercial office furniture, which is best suited to admin offices, reception desks, billing and scheduling teams, staff break rooms and provider offices. Exam and treatment rooms often have infection control and cleanability requirements that call for purpose-built medical furniture. Check with your infection prevention or compliance lead about any area patients use.

Can you handle old computers that stored patient information?

Computers and electronics removed during decommissioning are handled as e-waste with certificates of data destruction. Those certificates document what happened to the storage media. Your compliance team should confirm the documentation meets your own policies before the project starts.

How fast can a new clinic office be furnished?

Liquidation-grade furniture in stock can be ready in as little as 72 hours, and remanufactured furniture usually takes 7 to 14 days. New furniture typically takes 4 to 6 weeks. Space planning is free, so you can start the layout while the lease is still being finalized.

What does a billing office workstation cost?

A 6'x6' workstation, furniture only, is $615 liquidation-grade, $1,245 remanufactured or $1,920 new. Installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum. Prices exclude electrical and delivery.

Can your crews work when the clinic is closed?

Yes. Night and weekend crews are available so removal or installation does not interrupt patient schedules. We also coordinate freight elevators and loading docks in medical office buildings.
