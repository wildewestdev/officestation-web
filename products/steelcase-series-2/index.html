Fast, graded Steelcase seating

Used Steelcase Series 2 Chairs

When you need a group of adjustable task chairs seated fast, in-stock pre-owned Series 2 chairs are one of the quickest routes. Here is how grading, speed and ordering work.

5 minute read

Steelcase Series 2 is a modern, adjustable task chair built for everyday office work. Offices that refresh or consolidate often release them in groups, which makes Series 2 a practical target when you need matching chairs on a short timeline.

This page is built around speed and clarity: what each grade looks like, how quickly in-stock chairs can move, how to order a mixed-grade batch and what to confirm before chairs arrive.

What grades A, B and C look like on Series 2

Grade A, like new

Minimal or no visible wear. Back, seat, arms and base look close to new, and every adjustment works as designed. Choose A for reception, leadership offices and conference rooms where chairs are part of the first impression.

Grade B, very good

Light cosmetic wear such as small base scuffs or slightly worn arm pads. Fully functional. For most open office rows, Grade B delivers nearly the same daily experience as A at a lower cost.

Grade C, good

Visible wear on upholstery, arms or base, but the chair is sound and usable. Grade C is a strong choice for training rooms, overflow seating, shift work and any seat that sees heavy turnover.

Buy by zone, not by building. Most offices do not need Grade A everywhere. Assign grades by area: A for client-facing spaces, B for daily team seating, C for training and back-of-house. You get a polished look where it counts and keep budget for the rest of the project.

Planning a Series 2 order

A little prep gets your chairs moving faster and avoids mismatches on delivery day.

  • Count seats by zone. List how many chairs go in each area and which grade you want there.
  • Decide on must-match items. Color, arm style and back type are the details most likely to vary within a used batch.
  • Check flooring. Hard floors and carpet can call for different casters. Note your flooring so you can confirm compatibility.
  • Confirm the model label. Series 2 and Series 1 look similar. The label under the seat typically identifies the model.
  • Test a sample. On larger orders, ask about seeing or trying a sample chair before the full batch ships.
  • Plan the handoff. Know who receives the delivery, where chairs are staged and whether you want them unboxed and placed.

What to check when chairs arrive

Fast delivery only helps if the chairs are ready to use. Spend a few minutes on each one before your team sits down.

  1. Raise the seat, sit for a minute and make sure it holds height.
  2. Run the recline through its full range and test any lock positions.
  3. Adjust the arms in every direction they move.
  4. Roll the chair across your actual flooring.
  5. Compare color, arms and back type against your quote.
  6. Photograph any issue right away and send it to hello@officestation.com with your quote details.

Frequently asked questions

How fast can I get used Series 2 chairs?

Liquidation-grade chairs that are in stock can be ready in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured furniture usually takes 7 to 14 days, and new product runs 4 to 6 weeks. Quantity and matching requirements affect what is available right away.

What do grades A, B and C mean?

Grade A is like new with minimal wear. Grade B is very good with light cosmetic wear. Grade C is good, with visible wear but full usability. We grade each chair so you can choose the level that fits each area of your office.

Is the Steelcase warranty included?

Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized Steelcase dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, so plan on the grade, inspection and the terms on your quote as your protection.

Can I mix grades in one order?

Yes, and it is often the smartest way to buy. Put Grade A chairs where clients see them and Grade B or C chairs in back-office or training areas. List quantities per grade when you request your quote.

Do you deliver outside Phoenix?

Yes. Greater Phoenix delivery is $195 central or $295 metro. Anywhere else in the country, freight is quoted per project based on quantity and destination.
