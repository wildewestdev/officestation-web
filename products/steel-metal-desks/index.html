Used Steel Office Desks

Steel desks are among the most durable furniture an office can buy, which makes them one of the smartest things to buy used. Here is how we grade them, how fast you can get them and what delivery costs.

5 minute read

If you have ever tried to move an old steel tanker desk, you know why they last. Metal desks shrug off decades of daily use, and that durability is exactly why the used market for them is so good. The frame that took years of abuse in someone else's office usually has plenty left to give in yours.

This page covers the practical side: what styles you will find, how our condition grades apply to steel, how fast you can have desks in place and what it costs to get them delivered in Greater Phoenix.

Common styles of steel office desks

  • Double-pedestal desks. Drawer stacks on both sides, the classic full-size office desk. Heavy, stable and loaded with storage.
  • Single-pedestal desks. One drawer stack and open knee space on the other side. Good for tighter offices.
  • Steel desks with returns. An L-shaped setup that adds a side surface, handy for a second monitor or a printer.
  • Steel-frame tables and desks with laminate tops. A metal base with a wood-look or solid-color top. These fit modern spaces better than all-metal units.
  • Steel-frame benching. Shared long runs with metal legs and beams, used in open plans.

Our pre-owned inventory comes from brands such as Steelcase, Haworth, Allsteel, Kimball, Knoll, Herman Miller, Teknion, AIS, Humanscale and Friant, depending on what is available at the time.

Condition grades, applied to steel

Steel ages differently from wood or fabric. A desk can look rough and still be structurally perfect. Our grades are designed to tell you both things.

GradeSurface and finishStructure and drawers
A, like newMinimal to no visible wearSquare frame, smooth drawers, locks and keys where applicable
B, very goodLight scuffs or small marksSound frame, drawers open and close properly
C, goodVisible wear such as dings, scratches or worn top edgesStill sound and usable, drawers functional

For a warehouse office, shop floor or back-of-house team, Grade C steel is often the best value in the building. For a front office, Grade A or B keeps things looking sharp.

Ask about keys. Many steel pedestals have locks, and keys are the part most often lost when an office clears out. If locking drawers matter to your team, confirm key availability or plan to re-core the locks before you place the order.

What to check on a used metal desk

  1. Drawer slides. Pull every drawer all the way out. It should glide, stay level and close fully.
  2. File drawer hardware. If you need hanging files, check for file rails or bars in the deep drawer.
  3. Frame squareness. Set the desk on a flat floor and check for rocking. A bent leg or twisted frame is more serious than any scratch.
  4. Leveling glides. Missing glides scratch floors and cause wobble. They are inexpensive but worth noting.
  5. Top condition. Laminate tops can chip at the edges or delaminate. Metal tops can dent. Decide which cosmetic issues you can live with.
  6. Rust. Surface marks on steel stored in dry climates are rare, but look underneath and on the feet anyway.
  7. Dimensions and doorways. Double-pedestal steel desks are heavy and bulky. Measure doors, elevators and turns before delivery day.

Frequently asked questions

Why buy a used steel desk instead of a new one?

Steel desks are built from welded or bolted metal that rarely fails structurally, so a used unit often has most of its working life left. The wear you see is usually cosmetic, like scuffs, dings or a worn laminate top. That makes the savings over new easy to justify for most workspaces.

What do your condition grades mean for steel desks?

Grade A is like new, Grade B is very good with light wear, and Grade C is good with visible cosmetic wear but sound structure and working drawers. Ask about drawer function and frame condition on the specific desks in your quote.

How much is delivery in Phoenix?

Greater Phoenix delivery is $195 for central zones and $295 for the wider metro. Outside Greater Phoenix, freight is quoted per project because steel desks are heavy and cost depends on quantity and destination.

How quickly can I get used metal desks?

Liquidation-grade furniture that is in stock can be ready in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured pieces usually take 7 to 14 days. New product generally runs 4 to 6 weeks.

Can steel desks be refinished?

Steel desks often can be refreshed, and remanufactured tier furniture is where refreshed finishes are offered. Whether a given desk is a candidate depends on its condition and what is in stock, so ask us about options for your quantity.
