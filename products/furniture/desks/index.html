Need desks this week, not next month? Pre-owned designer desks are condition graded, priced below new, and in-stock pieces can be ready in as little as 72 hours.

When you need desks now

A lease starts earlier than planned. A team doubles in one quarter. A new satellite office has to open by the first of the month. In each case the problem is the same: a factory order will not arrive for 4 to 6 weeks, and people need somewhere to sit on day one.

Used office desks close that gap. Because pre-owned furniture already exists, in-stock liquidation-grade desks can be ready in as little as 72 hours. You skip the manufacturing queue, pay less than new, and keep good furniture in use.

Picking the right grade

Every used desk carries a condition grade. The grade is a trade-off between appearance and price, and function does not change between them.

  • Grade A, like new. Little or no visible wear. Choose it for reception, client meeting spaces and anywhere visitors spend time.
  • Grade B, very good. Light cosmetic signs of use. The everyday choice for most team areas.
  • Grade C, good. Noticeable cosmetic wear, fully functional. Ideal for call centers, warehouse offices, labs and temporary space.

You can mix grades in one order. Many buyers put A-grade desks up front and C-grade desks in back rooms, which lowers the total without changing how the office works.

How to get desks in 72 hours

The in-stock timeline depends as much on your request as on inventory. Follow these steps to keep an order on the fastest track:

  1. Give an exact count. Say how many desks you need and whether any must match.
  2. Send dimensions. Include the maximum width and depth each space allows.
  3. Be flexible where you can. Tell us which features are required and which are nice to have, such as finish color or drawer count.
  4. Share site details. Note the delivery address, dock or front door access, elevator availability and any building hours.
  5. Decide on installation. Say whether you want desks placed and assembled, so crews are scheduled with delivery.
  6. Approve quickly. In-stock pieces are first come, first served, so a fast yes holds your desks.
Flexibility buys speed. The more rigid the specification, the more likely you wait for remanufactured or new. If your team can accept a different laminate color or a similar model from another brand, in-stock options open up and the 72-hour window becomes realistic.

What to inspect on a used desk

Whether you are reading a listing or looking at photos, run through these points:

  • Stability. The desk should not rack side to side. Loose legs usually mean missing or stripped fasteners.
  • Surface. Check edge banding for peeling and tops for deep scratches, heat marks or water damage.
  • Drawers. Slides should run smoothly. Confirm keys for locking pedestals or plan for new lock cores.
  • Cable management. Look for grommets, trays or modesty panel cutouts where your power and data enter.
  • Sit-stand function. On adjustable desks, confirm motors, controls and cords, and remember electrical work is not included in furniture pricing.
  • Matching. If desks sit side by side, confirm they share a height, finish and edge profile.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can I get used office desks?

In-stock liquidation-grade desks can be ready in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured furniture usually takes 7 to 14 days, and new furniture typically takes 4 to 6 weeks. Your quote confirms the timing for your specific order.

What do the A, B and C condition grades mean?

Grade A is like new, with little or no visible wear. Grade B is very good, with light cosmetic signs of use. Grade C is good, with noticeable cosmetic wear but full function.

Which brands do your used desks come from?

Inventory changes constantly, but pre-owned desks can come from Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth, Humanscale, Teknion, Allsteel, Kimball, AIS and Friant. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture.

Do you deliver used desks outside Phoenix?

Yes. Greater Phoenix delivery is $195 in the central zone and $295 across the wider metro. Anywhere else in the country, freight is quoted per project.

Should I buy standalone desks or workstations?

Standalone desks suit private offices, small teams and layouts you expect to rearrange. Panel workstations suit larger teams that need privacy and built-in cable management. Free space planning can show both on your floor plan.
