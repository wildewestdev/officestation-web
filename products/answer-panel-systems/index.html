Used Steelcase Answer Cubicles

Answer panel workstations are one of the most common systems on the secondhand market. Here is how to buy them pre-owned with clear grades, honest pricing and realistic lead times.

6 minute read

If you are shopping for used Steelcase Answer cubicles, you are in good company. Answer is a panel-based system that was specified in a huge number of offices, which means pre-owned inventory turns up regularly when companies downsize, relocate or refresh. That availability is good news for your budget, but it also means quality varies a lot from one lot to the next. This page explains how Office Station sells pre-owned Answer workstations, what the two used tiers mean and what to check before you buy.

Independent reseller. Office Station sells pre-owned Answer components as an independent reseller, not an authorized Steelcase dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, which is why every station we quote carries a clear condition grade.

Why buyers look for pre-owned Answer panels

Panel systems like Answer are built from modular parts: panels, connectors, worksurfaces, storage and optional power and data components. Because the parts are modular, a used system can be reconfigured into a new footprint rather than reinstalled exactly as it came out of the previous office.

  • Cost. A remanufactured 6'x6' station costs about 35% less than new, and a liquidation-grade station costs about 68% less.
  • Speed. In-stock liquidation-grade stations can be ready in as little as 72 hours, versus 4 to 6 weeks for new.
  • Matching. If your office already runs Answer, pre-owned components can help you expand without introducing a second system.
  • Reuse. Buying used keeps functional furniture in service instead of in a landfill.

How condition grades work

Each lot is graded so you know what you are getting before it arrives.

  • Grade A, like new. Minimal visible wear. Suitable for any area of the office.
  • Grade B, very good. Light wear you notice up close, such as minor scuffs on edges or trim.
  • Grade C, good. Fully functional with visible wear. A practical choice where appearance matters less.

What to check before buying used Answer cubicles

Most problems with pre-owned panel systems come from mismatched parts, not worn ones. Use this checklist on any quote, including ours.

  1. Panel heights. Confirm the heights you need for privacy and sightlines. Lower panels open up a floor; taller panels help with focus and noise.
  2. Connectors and hardware. Ask whether every station includes the connectors needed for your layout, including L, T and end conditions. Missing hardware stalls installs.
  3. Generation compatibility. If you are adding to an existing Answer run, verify that parts from different production eras will connect cleanly before you order.
  4. Fabric and finish. Look at panel fabric, trim color and worksurface laminate together. Ask for photos of the actual lot, not a catalog image.
  5. Power and data. Find out whether powered components are included and what configuration they are. Hardwiring to building power requires a licensed electrician and is not part of the furniture price.
  6. Worksurfaces and storage. Check edge condition, surface depth, pedestal locks and whether keys are available.
  7. Local code and fire rules. Your building or jurisdiction may have requirements on egress widths and panel fabric. Your facilities team or landlord can confirm.

Free space planning for your layout

Before you buy, we will plan the footprint with you at no charge. Send a floor plan or rough dimensions, your headcount and any fixed features such as columns, windows and power locations. Planning first also helps you choose a mix. Many offices put remanufactured stations in visible areas and liquidation-grade stations in interior rows, which keeps the look consistent where it counts and saves money where it does not.

Frequently asked questions

Is Office Station an authorized Steelcase dealer?

No. Office Station is an independent reseller of pre-owned furniture and is not an authorized Steelcase dealer. Steelcase and Answer are trademarks of their owner. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture, so we describe condition with our own A, B and C grades.

How much does a used Answer 6x6 workstation cost?

A 6'x6' workstation, furniture only, runs $615 liquidation-grade or $1,245 remanufactured, compared with $1,920 for new. Those prices exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation. Volume pricing takes 5% off at 20+ stations, 8% at 50+ and 12% at 100+.

Can I add pre-owned Answer stations to the ones I already have?

Often yes, but it depends on the generation, panel heights, connectors and finishes of your existing run. Send us photos of your panels, connectors and any labels you can find, plus measurements, and we will check fit before you commit. Mixing generations is where most add-on problems come from.

How fast can used Answer cubicles be delivered?

Liquidation-grade stations in stock can be ready in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured stations usually take 7 to 14 days. In Greater Phoenix, delivery is $195 central or $295 metro, and freight elsewhere nationwide is quoted per project.

Do you install the workstations?

Yes. Professional installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum. Electrical hookup to building power is not included and should be handled by a licensed electrician.
