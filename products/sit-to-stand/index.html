Used Sit to Stand Desks

Height-adjustable desks carry motors, controllers and lift columns, so buying used means checking more than the surface. Here is how grading, lead times and installation work when you buy pre-owned sit to stand desks from Office Station.

6 minute read

A sit to stand desk is really two products: a worksurface and a lift system. The surface is easy to judge by eye. The lift system is where a used desk earns or loses its value. This page lays out how Office Station grades pre-owned height-adjustable desks, what they cost to get installed, and what you should check before you commit to a quantity.

What you are actually buying

Most commercial sit to stand desks fall into a few base types. Knowing which one you are looking at tells you a lot about how it will hold up.

  • Dual-motor electric bases. One motor per column. Smoother, better at carrying heavier loads like multiple monitors, and generally the most durable choice for all-day use.
  • Single-motor electric bases. One motor driving both columns through a shaft. Lighter duty and often slower, but fine for a single monitor and laptop setup.
  • Counterbalance or crank bases. No electricity required. Useful for spaces where you do not want cords, but adjustment takes more effort and they tend to see less frequent use.
  • Height-adjustable desks within workstation systems. Lift bases paired with panels or screens, often from a single manufacturer's system. These are best bought together so finishes and attachment hardware match.

We carry pre-owned furniture from brands including Steelcase, Herman Miller, Knoll, Haworth, Humanscale, Teknion, Allsteel, Kimball, AIS and Friant. Availability of any particular model changes with what comes in, so ask for current stock rather than assuming a specific base is on hand.

How condition grades work on a lift desk

Every piece we sell carries one of three grades. On a height-adjustable desk, the grade reflects both the surface and the base.

A cosmetic mark on a laminate top is a very different issue from a column that hesitates on the way up. When you review a quote, ask which grade applies to which component, especially if tops and bases came from different sources.

Warranty, stated plainly. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer for any manufacturer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. That matters more on a motorized desk than on a steel file cabinet, so factor it into your decision and ask us exactly what applies to your order.

Checklist before you buy used standing desks

Whether you are inspecting in person or reviewing photos and notes, run through this list.

  1. Full travel test. Run each desk from lowest to highest and back. Listen for grinding, clicking or uneven speed between columns.
  2. Controller and presets. Confirm the buttons respond and any memory presets save. A missing or cracked controller is a common gap.
  3. Height range. Check the minimum and maximum heights against your tallest and shortest users. Some bases do not go low enough for seated work at shorter heights.
  4. Power cords and control boxes. Make sure every desk has its power cord and control box. Mismatched parts can cause error codes.
  5. Wobble at full height. Lateral wobble at standing height is the most common complaint about lift desks. Crossbars and base width both affect it.
  6. Top size and mounting. Verify top dimensions and that the pre-drilled holes line up with the base. Swapping tops is possible but adds labor.
  7. Load needs. Count monitors, arms and any hardware you plan to mount. Match that to the base type.
  8. Power at the floor. Each electric desk needs a nearby outlet. Our furniture prices exclude electrical work, so plan any new circuits or power poles with your electrician.

Frequently asked questions

Are used sit to stand desks reliable?

A well-built commercial electric desk is designed for years of daily cycles, and many come out of offices with light use. Reliability depends on the base, so check the lift columns, motor noise and controller function before you buy. Every unit carries an A, B or C grade, and you can ask what condition notes apply to the desks you are considering.

Do manufacturer warranties transfer on a used height-adjustable desk?

Generally, no. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer, and manufacturer warranties usually do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Ask us what support applies to the specific desks in your quote before you order.

How fast can I get used standing desks?

In-stock liquidation-grade furniture can be ready in as little as 72 hours. Remanufactured pieces usually take 7 to 14 days, and new product typically runs 4 to 6 weeks. Actual availability depends on the quantity and matching you need.

Do you install sit to stand desks?

Yes. Professional installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum. Prices for the furniture itself exclude electrical, delivery and installation, so those appear as separate lines on your quote.

What does delivery cost?

In Greater Phoenix, delivery is $195 for central zones and $295 for the wider metro. Outside Greater Phoenix, nationwide freight is quoted per project based on quantity, weight and destination.
