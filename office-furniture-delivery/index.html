Office Furniture Delivery and Installation

Delivery and installation should not be a surprise on the final invoice. Here is exactly what it costs in Greater Phoenix, how nationwide freight is quoted, and what to prepare before the truck arrives.

6 minute read

When you compare office furniture quotes, delivery and installation are where the numbers tend to drift. One vendor bundles them, another leaves them vague, and a third adds a fuel charge at the end. Office Station publishes its rates so you can build a complete budget before you ask for a quote.

How the installation minimum works

The $95 per workstation rate has a $495 minimum. In practice:

  • 1 to 5 stations: $495 flat, since five stations at $95 comes to $475.
  • 6 stations: $570.
  • 12 stations: $1,140.
  • 40 stations: $3,800.

If you are just under the line, it can make sense to add the chair mats, pedestals or an extra station you were planning to buy later so the minimum is not wasted.

How nationwide freight is quoted

Outside Greater Phoenix, delivery is priced per project because the variables change too much for a flat rate. To get an accurate freight quote quickly, send:

  • The delivery address, including suite or floor
  • An item list: station count and size, chairs, desks, tables and storage
  • Building access details: dock or no dock, freight elevator, stairs or long carries
  • Your target date and any hours when deliveries are not allowed
  • Whether you want installation priced at the same time

A complete list up front avoids a second round of questions and gives you a number you can actually budget against.

What installation covers

Installation at $95 per workstation means a crew assembles your stations on site according to your layout: panels connected, work surfaces mounted and storage placed. It does not include connecting powered panels to building electrical, which is why electrical is listed separately. If you used free space planning, the crew works from that plan, which is the easiest way to avoid moving a run of stations after it is built.

Timing: when furniture can arrive

Delivery can only be as fast as the furniture is available. Lead times depend on the tier you choose:

  • Liquidation-grade: as little as 72 hours when in stock.
  • Remanufactured: usually 7 to 14 days.
  • New: 4 to 6 weeks.

If your move-in date is fixed, start from that date and work backward. Leave room for the electrician after installation, since stations with power panels are not usable until they are connected.

Tell us about the building before install day. Delivery delays often have nothing to do with the truck. They can come from a dock that was booked by another tenant, a freight elevator that was not reserved, or a certificate of insurance the property manager never received. Sort these out when you schedule, not the morning of.

Delivery day checklist

  1. Confirm the address and suite so the correct delivery zone is quoted.
  2. Ask property management about loading dock hours and freight elevator reservations.
  3. Request the certificate of insurance your building requires, with the right certificate holder listed.
  4. Check after-hours rules. Some buildings require furniture moves on nights or weekends.
  5. Clear the floor. Remove old furniture, boxes and anything sitting where stations will go.
  6. Mark the layout using your space plan so the crew knows where each run of stations lands.
  7. Schedule your electrician for after installation is complete.
  8. Walk the finished install and note any issues before the crew leaves.

Frequently asked questions

How much does office furniture delivery cost in Phoenix?

Greater Phoenix delivery is $195 for central zones and $295 for the wider metro. Your quote will show which zone applies to your address. Outside Greater Phoenix, freight is quoted per project.

How much does workstation installation cost?

Professional installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum. That means orders of five or fewer stations pay the $495 minimum, and six or more pay $95 each.

Is electrical hookup included in installation?

No. Workstation prices and installation exclude electrical. If your stations have powered panels, plan on a licensed electrician to make the connection to building power.

Do you deliver office furniture outside Arizona?

Yes. Delivery elsewhere in the country is handled as freight and quoted per project, based on what is shipping and where it is going. Share your address, item list and any building requirements to get an accurate number.

Can I install the furniture myself?

Installation is priced separately from furniture, so you can choose. Keep in mind that workstation systems involve panels, connectors and work surface supports, and a crew that installs them regularly will usually finish faster and with fewer missing parts.
