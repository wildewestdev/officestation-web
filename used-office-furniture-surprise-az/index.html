Pricing a growing Surprise team

Volume pricing is 5% at 20 or more stations, 8% at 50 or more and 12% at 100 or more. For a 30-station office, furniture-only totals look like this:

  • Liquidation-grade: 30 x $615 = $18,450, less 5% = $17,527.50
  • Remanufactured: 30 x $1,245 = $37,350, less 5% = $35,482.50
  • New: 30 x $1,920 = $57,600, less 5% = $54,720

Installation for 30 stations adds $2,850 at $95 each, and one Greater Phoenix delivery adds $195 or $295 depending on the zone confirmed for your address. Space planning is free, and it is worth using to lay out room for the hires you expect over the life of the new lease.

Mixing tiers on one order

Suppose 10 of those 30 stations sit where clients walk through and the other 20 are back office. Ten remanufactured stations at $1,245 and twenty liquidation-grade stations at $615 total $24,750 before any discount, which lands between the all-liquidation and all-remanufactured numbers above. Keep in mind that the remanufactured stations usually take 7 to 14 days, so the whole install should be scheduled around the longer lead time rather than splitting it into two visits and two delivery charges.

Fast walkthroughs for the space you are leaving

The old office is where time pressure usually hits. Your lease has an end date, and most leases expect the space back empty and clean. Office Station provides corporate office furniture liquidation and office decommissioning across Greater Phoenix, and Surprise is part of that market.

  1. Walkthrough. Typically within 48 hours in Greater Phoenix. Someone looks at what you have, its condition and how hard it is to remove.
  2. Written net offer. Within 5 business days. The offer equals resale value minus removal cost. Positive means you get paid.
  3. Removal. Crews can work nights and weekends so your team keeps working, with certificates of insurance for your landlord and dock or elevator coordination where needed.
  4. Handback. An itemized inventory, e-waste handled with certificates of data destruction, a photo-documented broom-clean space and a diversion summary.
Time the two jobs together. Schedule the walkthrough of your old space before you finalize the order for the new one. If some of your current furniture is worth keeping, you can move it instead of buying replacements, and whatever you do not keep goes into the net offer.

If you are relocating rather than just closing, corporate relocation can cover the move itself. You can get a rough idea of your offer first with the liquidation estimator.

Surprise move checklist

  • Read the surrender clause in your current lease so you know what condition the space must be in.

  • Schedule your electrician for any powered workstations.

Frequently asked questions

What are the workstation price tiers?

A 6'x6' workstation, furniture only, is $615 liquidation-grade, $1,245 remanufactured or $1,920 new. Prices exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation. Volume pricing takes 5% off at 20 or more stations, 8% at 50 or more and 12% at 100 or more.

How fast can someone walk through my current Surprise office?

Walkthroughs in Greater Phoenix, which includes Surprise, typically happen within 48 hours. After the walkthrough, you receive a written net offer within 5 business days.

How is a liquidation offer calculated?

The written net offer is the resale value of your furniture minus the cost to remove it. If the result is positive, Office Station pays you. Because the offer is in writing, you can see both numbers before you decide.

Can I use one tier for some areas and another for the rest?

Yes, and it is often the most cost-effective approach. Many offices put remanufactured stations in areas visitors see and liquidation-grade stations in back-office or overflow space. Confirm on your quote how volume pricing applies to a mixed order.

What does delivery and installation cost in Surprise?

Greater Phoenix delivery is $195 in the central zone or $295 in the metro zone, and your address sets the zone. Installation is $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum.
