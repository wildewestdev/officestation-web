Condition grades

Graded honestly, piece by piece

A

Like new

Minimal signs of use. Fabric, mesh and surfaces clean and intact; all mechanisms work smoothly.

B

Very good

Light, normal wear such as small scuffs or surface marks. Fully functional; cleaned and inspected.

C

Good

Visible wear or cosmetic blemishes. Fully functional and priced accordingly; a strong value for back-office use.

What to know about pre-owned inventory

  • Office Station is an independent reseller. We are not an authorized dealer of, or affiliated with, Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth, Humanscale or any other manufacturer named on this site. Brand and model names identify the original manufacturer only.
  • Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Any warranty Office Station offers is stated in writing on your quote or invoice.
  • Inventory changes daily and every item is subject to prior sale. Quantities, finishes and fabrics are confirmed at the time of your quote.
  • Photos may be representative of a model or product line rather than the exact unit. Condition grades are assigned at inspection and noted per item.
  • Pre-owned systems furniture from different installations may vary in finish, fabric and component generation; matched runs are quoted as matched.
  • Prices shown online are estimates for planning and do not include electrical, delivery or professional installation, which are quoted separately. Space planning is free. Final pricing, delivery windows and installation scope are confirmed in a written quote.

Questions

Pre-owned FAQ

Where does your pre-owned office furniture come from?

Most of it comes directly from corporate liquidations Office Station performs in the Phoenix metro. That's why inventory arrives in matched runs from single installations, and why it changes daily.

Is pre-owned Herman Miller or Steelcase furniture still covered by warranty?

Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned furniture. Office Station is an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer, and any warranty we offer is stated in writing on your quote.

What do the condition grades mean?

Grade A is like new, Grade B shows light normal wear, and Grade C has visible cosmetic wear but is fully functional. Every item is inspected and graded before it is quoted.

Can I see the furniture before buying?

Yes. Viewing appointments can be arranged for larger orders, and quotes list brand, model, grade and quantity for each line.

How quickly can pre-owned furniture be installed?

In-stock liquidation-grade furniture can be delivered and installed in as little as 72 hours in the Phoenix metro, depending on quantity and building access. Remanufactured furniture usually takes 7 to 14 days.
