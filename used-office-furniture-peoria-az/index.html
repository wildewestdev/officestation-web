Used Office Furniture in Peoria, AZ

Peoria offices can choose between three price tiers for the same 6'x6' workstation. This guide shows what each tier costs at different team sizes so you can pick the right one before you request a quote.

6 minute read

When you search for used office furniture in Peoria, AZ, most of what you find is a single price with little context. The more useful question is which tier fits your project. The same 6'x6' footprint comes in three versions at three price points, and the right choice depends on headcount, timeline and how the space will be used.

This page puts the numbers side by side, applies volume pricing at realistic Peoria team sizes and shows what else goes into the total.

Liquidation-grade

These are pre-owned stations from office liquidations, sold with a condition grade of A (like new), B (very good) or C (good). They are the lowest-cost and often the fastest option. Selection depends on what is in stock, so flexibility on brand and finish helps.

Remanufactured

Remanufactured stations cost about double liquidation-grade but still well under new. They make sense when you want a more consistent, refreshed appearance and can wait a week or two.

New

New stations take the longest and cost the most. Choose them when you need a particular finish or configuration that pre-owned inventory cannot reliably supply.

About brands and warranties. Pre-owned furniture from Herman Miller, Steelcase, Knoll, Haworth and other makers is sold by Office Station as an independent reseller, not an authorized dealer. Manufacturer warranties generally do not transfer to pre-owned pieces.

The rest of the cost

Furniture is the biggest line, but a realistic budget includes these as well:

  • Delivery: $195 for central Greater Phoenix addresses, $295 for metro addresses. Peoria is in the Greater Phoenix service area, and your quote confirms the zone for your address.
  • Installation: $95 per workstation with a $495 minimum. Twenty stations add $1,900. Fifty add $4,750.
  • Electrical: Not included. Powered panels need a licensed electrician to connect building feeds.
  • Space planning: Free. It confirms counts before you order, which protects your discount tier and avoids buying stations that do not fit.

Example: a 20-person Peoria office choosing remanufactured stations would pay $23,655 for furniture, $1,900 for installation and $195 or $295 for delivery, before electrical. That is a furniture-and-install total of roughly $25,750 to $25,850.

A quick way to choose

  1. Start with your date. If your team starts in under two weeks, liquidation-grade in stock is often the only tier that fits.
  2. Look at the room. Client-facing space may justify remanufactured or new. Back-office and training rooms rarely do.
  3. Check the count. Get close to a discount threshold before you finalize.
  4. Mix where it helps. Two tiers in one order is common and keeps the budget focused.

Three Peoria scenarios

Numbers are easier to use when they are attached to a real situation. Here is how the tiers tend to shake out for three common Peoria projects.

A 12-person office opening next week

Lead time decides this one. New stations take 4 to 6 weeks and remanufactured usually takes 7 to 14 days, so liquidation-grade in stock is the realistic choice. Twelve stations at $615 cost $7,380, plus $1,140 for installation and $195 or $295 for delivery.

A 35-person team with a month of runway

With time to spare and a count above 20, remanufactured stations earn the 5% discount: $41,396.25 in furniture. That keeps a consistent look without the new-furniture price of $63,840 at the same discount.

A 48-person expansion planned for next quarter

This team sits two stations below the 50-station tier. At 48 remanufactured stations with 5% off, furniture costs $56,772. At 50 stations with 8% off, it costs $57,270, just $498 more for two extra stations ready for future hires. Installation for the two extra stations adds $190, so compare the full quote before deciding.

Frequently asked questions

What are the three pricing tiers for workstations?

For a 6'x6' workstation, furniture-only pricing is $615 liquidation-grade, $1,245 remanufactured and $1,920 new. All three exclude electrical, delivery and professional installation.

How do volume discounts work for Peoria orders?

Volume pricing is 5% off at 20 or more stations, 8% at 50 or more and 12% at 100 or more. The discount shown on this page is applied to workstation furniture pricing, and your quote confirms the final numbers.

What does delivery to Peoria cost?

Peoria is in Greater Phoenix, where delivery is $195 for central addresses and $295 for metro addresses. Your quote confirms which zone applies to your address.

Which tier is fastest?

Liquidation-grade stations can be ready in as little as 72 hours when in stock. Remanufactured usually takes 7 to 14 days, and new takes 4 to 6 weeks.

Can I mix tiers in one order?

Yes, and many offices do. A common approach is remanufactured or new for visible areas and liquidation-grade for training rooms or overflow space.
